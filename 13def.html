The 13 Defence Pure

Defence exactly 13 — unlocks Black armour while maintaining pure-tier combat level efficiency

The 13 Defence Pure is built around a single gear unlock: Black armour requires exactly 10 Defence, but the optimal stop point is 13 Defence for access to the Black Full Helm (10 Defence) and Studded Leather items (20 Defence — which this build does NOT reach). The actual common target is stopping at 10–13 Defence to access black gear without meaningfully raising combat level.

More importantly, 13 Defence is often cited as the point where you gain access to certain cosmetic and gameplay unlocks without meaningfully impacting combat level. The primary competitive advantage: you fight in the same bracket as 1-Defence pures but take slightly less damage per hit, making extended fights marginally more survivable.

The honest assessment: the difference between 1 Defence and 13 Defence in a fight is small but real. Against other pures in the same combat bracket, the few extra defence levels reduce your opponent's hit frequency enough to matter in long grinding fights.

Why 13 Defence?

The specific unlocks at this Defence threshold

What 10–13 Defence Unlocks

  • Black armour (10 Defence) — slightly better than iron/steel, cosmetically distinct
  • Studded Leather (20 Defence) — NOT unlocked at 13. Requires 20 Defence.
  • Climbing Boots — No Defence requirement. Available regardless.

The honest reason most players choose 13 Defence is not gear-related — it's the marginal defensive improvement at the exact combat level cost. Training 13 Defence costs approximately 2–3 combat levels, which is a real trade-off from a 1-Defence pure's combat level efficiency.

Combat Level Impact

Defence adds 0.25 per level to combat above level 1. Going from 1 to 13 Defence adds: 12 × 0.25 = +3 combat levels. That's a real cost. For most players, the marginal survivability gain from 13 Defence isn't worth those 3 combat levels compared to staying at 1 Defence.

If you're choosing between a 1-Defence pure and a 13-Defence pure, strongly consider the 1-Defence route unless you have a specific reason for the extra defence. The 3 combat level cost at low brackets is significant — it shifts you into fights where opponents have 5–8 more HP.

Rotations

Identical to 60 Attack Pure or G Maul Pure depending on your spec weapon

The 13 Defence pure uses identical rotations to its equivalent melee pure build. If you have 60 Attack, follow the 60 Attack Pure rotations. If you're using a Granite Maul primarily, follow the G Maul Pure rotations.

The marginal 13 Defence levels add a small amount of damage reduction but don't change the fundamental strategy: chip with the primary weapon, spec at the right moment, combo eat at 35 HP.

Standard melee fight
Anglerfish overheal→Super Atk + Super Str→Engage with scimitar→Combo eat below 35 HP→G Maul / Claws spec close

Tips

  • 01
    Be clear-eyed about the trade-off. 13 Defence costs ~3 combat levels. That's real. The marginal survivability gain is small. Build this only if you have a specific reason.
  • 02
    The offensive setup is identical to 1-Def pures. Same gear, same spec weapons, same rotations. You're just slightly tankier.
  • 03
    Never train above 13 Defence. Beyond 13, you need 20 for Studded Leather, 40 for Dragon. Nothing useful at 14–19 or 21–39 for this bracket.
  • 04
    Consider whether 1 Defence serves you better. In most cases the 3 saved combat levels from 1 Defence let you fight opponents with less HP, which compensates for the marginal defence advantage.
