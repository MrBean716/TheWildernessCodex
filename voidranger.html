Void Ranger

Elite Void Ranger Helm — ranged-primary Void Pure focused on maximising Blowpipe output

The Void Ranger is a Void Pure that plays primarily in the ranged style. While the full Void Pure (void.html) switches between melee and ranged using the one-helm swap, the Void Ranger specialises in the ranged setup — staying on the Void Ranger Helm and Blowpipe as much as possible, switching to melee only as a prayer bypass when opponents activate Protect from Missiles.

The appeal is simplicity relative to full hybrid void play: instead of constant back-and-forth style switching, you commit to ranged as your primary and melee as your reactive switch. This makes the build more accessible than the full void hybrid while retaining most of the damage output.

Elite Void Ranger Helm + Elite Void Top + Elite Void Robe + Void Gloves = +10% ranged accuracy and +12.5% ranged damage. At 99 Ranged with Dragon Darts and Rigour, this produces one of the highest ranged DPS outputs per combat level in the game.

Lesson: Staying on Blowpipe through their Protect Melee dealt 101 ranged damage while they scrambled to react. When they switched to Protect Missiles, the one-helm melee swap was instant. The MSB spec closed a fight already decided.

Tips

  • 01
    Stay on Blowpipe as long as it's dealing damage. The Void Ranger identity is ranged-primary. Don't switch to melee unless Protect Missiles is active.
  • 02
    The one-helm swap is the entire advantage. Swapping only the Void Ranger/Melee helm keeps style switches within 1 tick. Never swap body, legs, or gloves.
  • 03
    Rigour adds +23% ranged damage — always active. Ranged DPS without Rigour is significantly lower. Rigour requires 74 Prayer.
  • 04
    Monitor Blowpipe scales. 500+ per trip minimum. Running out removes your primary weapon.
  • 05
    Full Void Pure guide covers deeper hybrid play. If you want to add more style switching complexity, see void.html for the complete hybrid rotation.
