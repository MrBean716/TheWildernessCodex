The Obby Mauler Philosophy

Why 1 Attack, 1 Defence, 99 Strength is the most asymmetric account in OSRS PvP

The Obby Mauler — short for Obsidian Mauler — is the most radically streamlined PvP build in OSRS. Its design is pure reduction: strip every stat that isn't Strength to its absolute minimum, put all levelling investment into a single offensive number, and use a weapon that requires no Attack level to wield.

The result is a character at combat level 40–50 that hits like a level 90 account. Opponents in its combat range have 30–45 HP at most. The Obby Mauler has 99 Strength. The disparity is the strategy.

The Three Pillars

  • 1 Attack — never trained. Keeps combat level as low as mathematically possible. The Granite Maul requires no Attack level — it is the only viable melee spec weapon usable at 1 Attack.
  • 1 Defence — never trained. Keeps combat level low. The account wears leather armour or the Obsidian set, which has no Defence requirement.
  • 99 Strength — the sole investment. Every resource, every hour spent training, goes here. At 99 Strength with maximum Strength bonus gear, the Granite Maul special attack can hit 57 damage in a single hit.

The Combat Level Arithmetic

Combat level is calculated from Attack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints, Prayer, Magic, and Ranged. With 1 Attack, 1 Defence, and 99 Strength, the base melee combat contribution is:

  • Melee CB = (Attack + Strength) × 0.325 = (1 + 99) × 0.325 = 32.5
  • Total CB ≈ 32 before adding HP and Prayer contributions
  • With 1 Prayer and base 10 HP: combat level ≈ 36
  • With 52 Prayer and 70 HP: combat level ≈ 52

At combat 40, opponents have 35–45 HP. Your 99 Strength makes the Granite Maul capable of one-shotting many of them with a single special attack. This is the foundational power of the build.

The Obsidian armour set (Obsidian helm, platebody, platelegs) provides a unique set bonus when worn together: +10% melee accuracy and +10% melee damage when wielding an Obsidian weapon. Combined with the Granite Maul (also from TzHaar), this bonus applies to both the auto-attack and the special attack — making Obsidian gear the best-in-slot choice for Obby Mauler builds, despite having no Defence requirement and being usable at any Defence level.

Limitations to Be Aware Of

The Obby Mauler is not without serious limitations. It fights in the lowest combat bracket — which means most encounters are against F2P players, low-level pures, and low-level main accounts. The bracket is very dependent on finding prey: the account is devastating against the right targets and largely irrelevant against defence accounts with prayer and 99 HP. Understanding who to fight and who to walk away from is the primary skill of an Obby Mauler player.

The Obby Mauler is not a strong Wilderness progression account. It dominates in edge PKing and F2P worlds but has very limited applications in mid-to-deep Wilderness where opponents regularly have 75+ HP and protection prayers. Know your theatre of operation before committing to this build.

Granite Maul Mechanics

The 0-tick special attack explained in full — how it works, why it's unique, and how to execute it correctly

What Makes the G Maul Special

Every other special attack in OSRS fires at the beginning of your next attack cycle — you activate the spec bar and wait for your character's next swing, which then deals the spec hit. The Granite Maul is different. Its special attack fires as a zero-tick additional attack on top of your existing attack cycle, not instead of it.

This means: on the tick your character swings for any reason, you can right-click the Granite Maul and select "Use Special Attack" to fire an additional Granite Maul hit on the same (or immediately following) tick. The opponent receives two hits so close together they cannot eat between them.

The 0-Tick Technical Explanation

In OSRS, a "0-tick attack" means the attack happens on tick 0 — the same tick the interaction was triggered. Normally your character's weapon attacks on a fixed cycle (the whip attacks every 4 ticks, the scimitar every 4 ticks). The G Maul spec inserts an additional hit outside this cycle, with zero ticks of delay from when you right-click it.

The practical result is the "G Maul stack" — the combination of a preceding auto-attack or weapon hit followed immediately by one or two G Maul specs arriving so fast that the opponent's response window is effectively zero.

The G Maul spec does not use a percentage-based special attack bar in the traditional sense. It has its own charge system — each spec drains approximately 50% of its special energy bar, which means you can fire two specs in rapid succession (using 100% of the maul's own bar), then wait for it to recharge before speccing again. The recharge is roughly 30 seconds for a full bar.

The Two-Spec Burst

The defining technique of the Obby Mauler is firing two G Maul specs back to back in rapid succession:

Why Opponents Cannot Eat Through It

Eating food takes one game tick (0.6 seconds). The G Maul double spec fires two hits within approximately one game tick total — or at worst across two consecutive ticks. Against a target at 50 HP, two specs hitting 28 and 26 total 54 damage. The target would need to eat two separate food items in two consecutive ticks to survive — and their first eat only heals 20 HP, meaning if the second spec arrives before their second eat, they die.

At the Obby Mauler's combat level, opponents have 30–45 HP. A single G Maul spec at 99 Strength can frequently one-shot them outright. This is not a corner case — it is the standard outcome.

The Obsidian Set Bonus

Wearing the full Obsidian set (Obsidian helm, platebody, platelegs) while wielding an Obsidian weapon gives +10% melee accuracy and +10% melee damage. The Granite Maul counts as an Obsidian weapon for this purpose. Combined with 99 Strength and the Berserker Necklace, the damage output is the highest available at 1 Attack level.

Berserker Necklace Interaction

The Berserker Necklace gives +20% damage specifically when wielding an Obsidian weapon — including the Granite Maul. This is a multiplicative damage boost on top of the Obsidian set bonus. At 99 Strength, Berserker Necklace is the single most impactful amulet slot item for this build. It does not improve accuracy — the +20% is purely damage. Since the G Maul already has high base accuracy at 99 Strength against low-defence targets, this trade is worthwhile.

The Berserker Necklace + Obsidian set + G Maul spec is the maximum damage configuration for this build. At 99 Strength with a Super Strength Potion active, the G Maul spec maximum hit reaches approximately 57 damage. Against opponents with 35–45 HP, a single spec frequently ends the fight.

Every stat you add beyond the core raises your combat level, shifting you into a bracket with healthier, better-geared opponents. This is a trade-off you must consciously decide.

Hitpoints Management

HP is the most interesting stat to manage on an Obby Mauler. HP levels increase passively as you gain Strength XP — you cannot avoid it. But you can manage how much HP you have through your training method:

  • Nightmare Zone (NMZ) — train Strength here for the best XP/hour. NMZ gives 4:1 Strength to HP XP ratio when using the Absorption potion method — you minimise HP gain while maximising Strength gains.
  • Pest Control — also gives combat XP at a reduced HP rate. Another option for minimising HP gains.
  • Normal combat — gives a 1:3 ratio of HP to Strength XP. Significantly raises your HP faster, pushing your combat level up.
Some Obby Mauler players deliberately keep HP low (30–40 HP) to maintain the lowest possible combat level. This is a valid strategy but makes every fight extremely risky — you can be one-shot by most opponents you face. The trade-off is fighting people with 20–30 HP who the G Maul kills reliably in one spec. Most competitive builds balance HP around 50–60.

Why No Dragon Items?

Dragon equipment generally requires higher stats to wield or wear. Dragon boots require 60 Defence. Dragon platelegs require 60 Defence. Dragon gloves (as part of Barrows Gloves system) require Recipe for Disaster completion which is incompatible with a 1-Defence account at meaningful gear levels. The Obsidian set specifically exists to fill this void for low-requirement characters — it provides real defensive stats and the set bonus makes it offensively superior to anything else available at 1 Defence.

Matchup Guide

Who to fight, who to avoid, and how to handle every common opponent at ultra-low combat

Your Target Profile

The Obby Mauler is a specialist. It does not fight everything in the Wilderness — it hunts specific target types where its extreme Strength-to-combat-level ratio creates an overwhelming advantage. Before engaging anyone, mentally check three things:

  1. Do they have more than 45 HP? If yes, your specs might not one-shot, and you need to assess their food supply.
  2. Do they have protection prayers active? If yes and you have no Ranged switch, walk away.
  3. Do they have significantly more food than you? If yes, you will lose an extended fight at 1 Defence.

vs. F2P Players and Low-Level Mains

Highly favourable — these are your ideal targets. F2P players and low-level mains in the Wilderness rarely have protection prayers, typically carry 3–5 food items at most, and have 30–45 HP at the combat levels you'll encounter them. The G Maul double spec frequently kills in one to two ticks.

  • Engage immediately — don't hesitate or they'll log out
  • Spec in the first tick — delay gives them time to eat or run
  • Overheal is critical — their scimitars hit reliably through 1 Defence

vs. Low-Level Pures (Similar Combat Level)

Coin flip — depends on HP and food. A 60 Attack pure at combat 45 has similar combat level but potentially higher HP (if they've trained more) and better auto-attack capability with the Dragon Scimitar. The fight comes down to who specs first and who overhealed more.

  • Spec immediately on engagement — delay is always bad for you
  • Anglerfish overheal is the deciding factor in mirror-bracket fights
  • If they have a prayer book open, expect prayer blocking

vs. Prayer-Using Opponents

Hard counter without the Ranged switch — mostly avoidable with it. An opponent with Protect from Melee active takes 0 damage from your G Maul specs. This completely shuts down your primary strategy. Without a Ranged switch, your only options are to leave or wait for their prayer to drain naturally (very slow at low combat).

  • Core Obby Mauler (no Ranged): simply walk away from anyone with overhead prayer
  • Ranged Switch Mauler: MSB spec through their Protect Melee, then G Maul when they drop it
  • If they're consistently praying, they've specifically prepared for you — don't give them the satisfaction of an extended fight

vs. High-Defence Accounts (75+ Defence)

Unfavourable — high defence reduces your spec's damage meaningfully. Against 75 Defence with full rune or dragon armour, your G Maul spec max hit is reduced and accuracy is lower. The fight may drag out past your spec bar recharge, at which point your 1 Defence becomes the deciding factor.

  • Only engage if they have low HP and minimal food
  • Two spec volleys (4 specs total across two full bar charges) should close most fights even with high defence if you can survive that long
  • If they have 60+ HP and full food, leave

vs. Higher-Level Accounts (75+ Combat)

Generally avoid. Accounts 30+ combat levels above you have significantly higher HP (70–99 HP), better gear, potentially Piety, and the resources to outlast you at 1 Defence. An occasional lucky kill is possible, but this is not your hunting ground.

Any player with 75+ combat and full food supply will outlast an Obby Mauler in an extended fight. Your 1 Defence means every hit they land at 75 Strength or above deals near-full damage. The Obby Mauler dominates its specific bracket — outside that bracket, run.

F-Keys & Interface Setup

The G Maul spec is a right-click action — here's the exact sequence and how to execute it reliably under pressure

The Obby Mauler has the simplest F-key setup of any build in this codex — but the one action that matters, the G Maul right-click spec, must be executed faster and more reliably than anything else in OSRS PvP. A 0.3-second delay costs you the fight.

Where to Right-Click: Equipment Slot vs Inventory

You have two options for right-clicking the G Maul:

  • Equipment slot (weapon tab) — the G Maul appears in your weapon slot. Right-clicking this is the fastest method because it doesn't require opening your inventory first. Requires F5 to be on the Equipment tab, then right-clicking the weapon graphic.
  • Inventory slot — if you keep the G Maul in a fixed inventory slot, you can F2 (inventory) then right-click it there. This is slightly slower but more comfortable for players used to clicking inventory items.

Try both methods in Clan Wars and choose the one that produces more consistent results under pressure. Most experienced Obby Mauler players right-click the weapon in the equipment slot.

Inventory Organisation

  • Slots 1–2 (top left) — Anglerfish and Sharks adjacent to each other. First thing you click before and during fights.
  • Slots 3–4 — Karambwans adjacent to sharks for instant combo-eat access.
  • Slot 5 — Super Strength Potion. Used once at fight start. Consistent position.
  • Slot 6 — Prayer Potion (if using). Adjacent to Strength potion.
  • Slot 7 — Stamina Potion. Escape tool, clicked deliberately.
  • If using Ranged switch — MSB (i) and arrows in fixed adjacent slots. Right side of inventory for clarity separation from food.
Right-clicking in OSRS opens the context menu at your cursor position. The "Use Special Attack" option will always be in the same position in the menu for the Granite Maul. With enough Clan Wars practice, your right hand learns the motion — right-click, move mouse down two options, left-click — without conscious thought. This muscle memory is the entire mechanical skill of the Obby Mauler.

Speed Drill — Clan Wars Practice

The specific movement to drill is: engage opponent → right-click weapon slot → click Use Special Attack → right-click weapon slot → click Use Special Attack. Repeat this 100 times on a stationary target in Clan Wars until your hands do it automatically at speed. The window is approximately 1.2 seconds (two game ticks) to complete both specs before an opponent with fast reactions can eat through the first one.

Tips & Common Mistakes

What separates a skilled Obby Mauler from someone who simply has 99 Strength

The Obby Mauler Commandments

  • 01
    Never train Attack or Defence. Ever. One Attack level raises your combat from ~42 to ~55. One Defence level raises it similarly. Both reduce the core advantage of the build: fighting far below your actual damage potential. Verify every XP reward, quest lamp, and training method before committing. This cannot be undone.
  • 02
    Anglerfish overheal before every single fight. At 50 HP base, a lucky 40-hit from an opponent with 65 Strength kills you outright. At 72 HP (overhealed), the same hit leaves you at 32 HP — survivable with a combo eat. The Anglerfish is not comfort food — it's the margin of safety that lets your specs land without dying on the same tick.
  • 03
    Super Strength Potion before every fight, no exceptions. At 99 Strength, a Super Strength sip raises your effective Strength by ~14 levels and adds approximately 4–6 max hit to the G Maul spec. The difference between a 47 max hit and a 53 max hit is the difference between a one-spec kill and needing two specs. Pot up before you spec.
  • 04
    Spec immediately on engagement. Unlike zerker or 1-def pure accounts that have a "grind phase," the Obby Mauler should spec as fast as possible after the fight starts. Your best window is the first tick — before the opponent has activated any prayer or eaten any food. Delay helps them, not you.
  • 05
    Walk away from anyone with Protect from Melee active. Your G Maul deals zero damage through the prayer. Unless you have a Ranged switch, there is nothing to be done. Recognise the prayer icon above their head and disengage. Fighting into Protect Melee as a no-Ranged Obby Mauler is a guaranteed waste of time and food.
  • 06
    Know your spec bar status at all times. After two specs, your G Maul needs ~30 seconds to fully recharge. If you engage a second fight immediately after depleting the bar, you have no finishing tool. Between fights, check the spec bar. Wait for a full recharge before hunting the next target.
  • 07
    The Berserker Necklace + Obsidian set + G Maul interaction is non-negotiable. Don't substitute the Berserker Necklace for an Amulet of Torture. The Amulet of Torture gives +15 Strength bonus (a flat add). The Berserker Necklace gives +20% Obsidian weapon damage (a multiplier applied after all bonuses). At 99 Strength, the multiplicative Berserker Necklace adds more total damage than the additive Torture.
  • 08
    Train Strength via NMZ for minimum HP gains. NMZ Absorption method gives 4:1 Strength to HP XP. If you train on normal monsters, you get a 3:1 HP to Strength ratio — meaning far more HP (and thus combat levels) per Strength level. NMZ is slower XP/hour but results in a lower final combat level, which is the entire point of the build.
  • 09
    Escape after failed specs — don't grind. The Obby Mauler is not a sustained DPS account. Without specs, your G Maul auto-attack at 1 Attack is slower to kill than even a rune scimitar. If your double spec fails and the opponent has food left, the correct play is almost always to escape and recharge, not to keep swinging.
  • 10
    Pick your fights carefully. The Obby Mauler is the most specialised account in this codex — it dominates a narrow range of fights. Learn to identify the specific matchups you win (low-HP, low-food, no-prayer opponents in the 30–55 combat range) and ignore everything outside that range. Discipline in target selection is the meta-skill of this build.

Mistakes That Kill Obby Maulers

Accidentally training Attack or Defence. Even one level of either permanently raises your combat level and reduces the build's core advantage. Never accept an XP reward without verifying the skill. Never train on NPCs without confirming your attack style is set to "Strength" not "Controlled" (Controlled trains Attack and Defence as well). Check the attack style panel every session.
Engaging without overheal. Fighting at base 50 HP makes you vulnerable to a single good hit from any opponent. Overheal to 72 HP with Anglerfish before every fight. This is not optional.
Spec reuse before the bar recharges. After two G Maul specs, attempting to spec again before the bar is full will fire a non-spec auto-attack instead — but many players mistake this for a spec and wonder why the hit was lower. Wait for the bar (check F1 or the weapon slot). A full recharge takes about 30 seconds.
Using Amulet of Torture instead of Berserker Necklace. The Torture's flat +15 Strength is outperformed by the Berserker Necklace's +20% multiplicative Obsidian bonus at 99 Strength. This is counterintuitive because the Torture is the "better" amulet on most builds — but the unique Obsidian interaction makes the Berserker Necklace superior specifically for this account.
Fighting in the wrong bracket. An Obby Mauler at combat 42 fighting a 90 CB account with 99 HP and Bandos is not a winnable fight under any conditions. Know your target bracket — it's narrow, but inside it, the build is devastating.

Where to Fight

The best locations for Obby Mauler PKing and the correct approach for each environment

Clan Wars — Practice First

Drill the G Maul right-click double spec until it's completely automatic. You need to be able to fire both specs within two game ticks reliably. In Clan Wars, focus specifically on:

  • The right-click → "Use Special Attack" → right-click → "Use Special Attack" sequence at maximum speed
  • Overheal + pot timing before engaging
  • Combo eating from 25 HP or below

The mechanical complexity of the Obby Mauler is low compared to other builds, but the execution of the G Maul spec must be flawless. A fumbled right-click that fires a non-spec auto-attack instead of a spec costs you fights that should have been clean kills.

Edgeville F2P Worlds — Primary Hunting Ground

The Obby Mauler's natural habitat. F2P Edgeville Wilderness attracts low-level players who:

  • Have 30–45 HP — prime G Maul spec range
  • Rarely use protection prayers
  • Carry 3–5 food items, not 10–12
  • Don't expect to be one-shot by a combat 42 account

The surprise factor is real — players looking at your combat level expect manageable damage. They see combat 42 and don't activate prayers. Your G Maul spec hits for 35–50 before they've processed what's happening.

P2P Edgeville (Low-Level Wilderness)

Members worlds have more varied opponents but also more lucrative kills. Low-level accounts training prayer, questing, or clue-scrolling in low Wilderness are excellent targets — they often carry noted supplies or valuable items for their quest and have minimal combat preparation.

Be aware that members worlds have many more accounts with protection prayers at all combat levels — the Prayer Mauler variant or Ranged Switch becomes more necessary in P2P compared to F2P where prayer usage is rarer at low levels.

Bounty Hunter

Bounty Hunter matches you with a target near your combat level. For an Obby Mauler at CB 42–52, targets are consistently in the low-level bracket where the build excels. BH points also buy Magic Shortbow (i) imbuements — useful if building the Ranged Switch variant. Good risk-free practice environment before F2P edge PKing.

Last Man Standing (LMS)

LMS isn't ideal for the Obby Mauler because standardised gear means no Obsidian set bonus — the build's primary advantage is neutered. However, LMS is still useful for practicing the right-click spec sequence and combo eating under pressure. The mechanics transfer even if the damage numbers don't.

Who NOT to Hunt

  • Anyone with 75+ combat — they have too much HP and too much food supply
  • Players visibly using overhead protection prayers — Protect Melee = 0 damage from your specs. Leave unless you have Ranged switch
  • High-level PKers hunting for kills — they're looking for you specifically and are prepared for your strategy
  • Accounts with visibly expensive gear — implies high bankroll, full food, and prayer awareness

Building the Account — Recommended Path

  1. Strength to 70–80 via controlled methods (NMZ or Pest Control to minimise HP gain)
  2. Obtain Obsidian set — buy from GE or TzHaar shop. No requirements. Do this early.
  3. Obtain Berserker Necklace — buy from GE. Significant damage upgrade.
  4. Strength to 99 — NMZ Absorption method. Slow but minimal HP gain.
  5. Decide on Prayer level — 1 (lowest CB) vs 31–44 (protection prayers, a few CB levels higher)
  6. Drill G Maul spec in Clan Wars
  7. Begin F2P edge PKing — start with cheap gear until mechanics are solid

Connecting to Other Builds

The Obby Mauler is a terminal build for most players — its design is so specialised that "graduating" to another build requires essentially starting a new account. If you want to explore other PvP styles, the 1-Defence Pure guide is the natural next step — the G Maul spec knowledge transfers directly, and training Attack to 60 opens far more options without completely changing the core philosophy. The two builds share the 1 Defence ceiling and the G Maul finishing mechanic; the difference is just the addition of a Dragon Scimitar for sustained damage.
