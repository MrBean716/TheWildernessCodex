Skiller PK Build

0 combat stats trained — PvP as a pure skiller

A Skiller PK Build is a pure skiller account (no combat stats trained beyond their starting values) that enters the Wilderness. This is a curiosity and challenge build rather than a competitive PvP account — a skiller has virtually no offensive capability. The appeal is entirely in the roleplay, the challenge, and the occasional accidental kill via environmental damage or specific mechanics.

A true skiller has 1 Attack, 1 Strength, 1 Defence, 1 Ranged, 1 Magic, and 10 Hitpoints (the starting value). They deal minimal damage but can still enter the Wilderness and technically participate in PvP.

What a Skiller Can Actually Do in PvP

  • Cast binds/entangles via Magic — if they've trained Magic through non-combat methods (lunar spells, etc.). A skiller can have 99 Magic without any combat XP from combat spells.
  • Vengeance — Skiller accounts with high Lunar Magic can cast Vengeance, reflecting damage. A skiller who gets hit for 50 reflects 37 via Veng.
  • Environment and consumables — Poison from weapons, freezes, and environmental hazards apply independently of combat stats.
  • Revenants — Fighting Revenants in the Wilderness still provides some income potential.
The most viable 'Skiller PK' strategy is Lunar Magic Vengeance spam. Getting hit hard while Vengeance is active reflects ~75% of incoming damage. Against opponents who spec for large hits, a Veng skiller reflects significant damage passively.

Tips & Reality Check

  • 01
    This is not a competitive PvP build. Be clear-eyed about this. A skiller enters the Wilderness as a challenge/roleplay experience, not expecting to win fights against prepared opponents.
  • 02
    Vengeance is your most powerful tool. Get hit, reflect 75% back. Against specing opponents this is the best offensive output a skiller can achieve.
  • 03
    Lunar Magic training doesn't give combat XP. You can reach 99 Magic through Lunar spellbook (Humidify, Plank Make, etc.) without gaining a single Magic combat XP. This is how skillers access Vengeance.
  • 04
    Pray up before anything else. Protect from Melee reduces incoming damage significantly. A skiller relying on Veng needs to survive the hit to reflect it.
  • 05
    The real reward is the experience and community response. Most PvP players are surprised and amused by a skiller in the Wilderness. That's the actual content here.
