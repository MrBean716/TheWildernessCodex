40 Defence Build

Rune armour tier and Black d'hide — balanced defensive upgrade

The 40 Defence Build trains Defence to exactly 40 — unlocking Rune armour and Black d'hide armour (40 Ranged + 40 Defence). This is one of the most significant defensive jumps in the game — Rune provides a full tier above Adamant, and Black d'hide is the go-to ranged armour for builds below the Dragon/Karil's tier.

Many players treat 40 Defence as the natural "budget mid-level" stopping point. You gain real defensive capability without committing to the full 75 Defence Bandos path.

Rotations

Same as equivalent pure/zerker builds — 40 Defence changes survivability, not rotation

A 40 Defence build uses the same combat rotations as its equivalent melee configuration. See the relevant Attack-level guide for rotations. 40 Defence primarily changes how long you survive during the grind phase, not how the spec close works.

Tips

  • 01
    Helm of Neitiznot is the best helm at exactly 40 Defence. It's strictly better than Rune Full Helm for combat.
  • 02
    Black d'hide Body requires 70 Ranged as well as 40 Defence. Train Ranged to 70 to unlock the best ranged armour at this tier.
  • 03
    5 more Defence levels unlocks the Berserker Helm. Many builds train to 45 Defence specifically for the Berserker Helm's +3 Str bonus. Decide whether the extra CB levels are worth it.
  • 04
    Dragon Boots require 60 Defence — still 20 levels away. Use Granite Boots (+4 Str, 50 Str req) as your best boot option at 40 Defence.
