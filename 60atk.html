The 60 Attack Pure

The balanced 1-Defence pure — Dragon Scimitar sustained DPS meets Dragon Claws burst spec

The 60 Attack Pure is the most popular 1-Defence pure build in OSRS. 60 Attack unlocks two crucial weapons: the Dragon Scimitar for sustained melee DPS and the Dragon Claws for one of the most powerful burst specs in the game. At 99 Strength and combat ~78, you hit opponents who have 60–75 HP — and the Dragon Claws spec can deal 60–80 total damage in four simultaneous hits.

Unlike the G Maul Pure, the Dragon Claws spec has a unique advantage: its four-hit spread makes it extremely difficult to prayer-block (even if prayed, the secondary hits still land), and its fast animation makes it harder for opponents to eat between the initial hit and the follow-up.

Dragon Claws deal four hits: the first roll, then 50% of it, then 25%, then another 25%. If your first hit is 32, the total is 32+16+8+8 = 64. At 99 Strength on a 1-Defence opponent, first hits of 30–40 are common.

Dragon Claws Spec Mechanic

Dragon Claws use 50% of the spec bar per use. The spec fires four hits. Hit 1 is a normal damage roll. Hit 2 = half of Hit 1. Hit 3 = half of Hit 2. Hit 4 = same as Hit 3. This means the spec's total output scales heavily with the first hit.

Dragon Claws close
Opponent at ~55 HP, food low→Equip Dragon Claws→F1 → Spec Bar→Click opponent→4 hits land
A first hit of 34 produces: 34+17+8+8 = 67 total. Against a 1-Defence opponent with ~55 HP who has eaten their last food, this is a reliable kill. The four-hit spread means even Protect from Melee only reduces one component.

Fight Examples

Five annotated fights for the 60 Attack Pure

I
vs. 1-Def Pure — Scimitar + Claws
Victory
Mirror bracket fight. You (combat 80) vs SwordFury (combat 78, Dragon Scimitar, Granite Maul).
You pot up, engage. Scimitar: 29, 24, 33. SwordFury has eaten 3 sharks.
SwordFury G Maul specs you: 28 + 21. You combo eat — back to 75 HP.
SwordFury's spec bar is empty. 2 food left, ~50 HP.
You Dragon Claws: 31+15+7+7 = 60. Kill from 50 HP.
Lesson: Surviving the G Maul double spec (Anglerfish overheal helped) and waiting until their spec was gone before firing your own Claws is the perfect sequence. Your Claws cost 50% spec bar but arrived when they had no response.
II
vs. Zerker — Punching Up
Victory
You (1-Def, combat 80) vs ZerkerBoss (45 Def, combat 88, Dragon Scimitar, Dragon Claws).
You open with MSB (i) spec — ZerkerBoss activates Protect Missiles too late: 20 + 24.
ZerkerBoss activates Protect Melee. You switch back to scimitar and swap prayer too.
Trade hits — their 45 Defence reduces your scimitar hits to ~18 average. Longer grind needed.
ZerkerBoss Dragon Claws spec: 26+13+6+6 = 51. You survive on 24 HP — Anglerfish overheal.
You combo eat. Spec bar intact. Their spec is gone. Dragon Claws: 34+17+8+8 = 67. Kill.
Lesson: Their higher Defence made the grind phase harder, but the MSB opener dealt 44 unprotected damage before prayer activated. Surviving their Claws with overheal, then deploying your own Claws after theirs was spent, reversed the gear disadvantage.
III
vs. Prayer Tank — Full Prayer Bypass
Victory
Opponent activates Protect from Melee at fight start and never drops it.
PrayBot Protect Melee active. Scimitar and Claws deal 0.
You MSB (i) spec: 18 + 22 = 40. Their prayer doesn't block ranged.
PrayBot switches to Protect Missiles. You switch to Scimitar.
Scimitar: 29. They're at ~25 HP. One food left.
Dragon Claws: 27+13+6+6 = 52. Kill from 25 HP.
Lesson: Prayer switching is the 60 Attack Pure's solution to prayer tanks. MSB forces them into a choice: drop Protect Melee (and take scimitar hits) or switch to Protect Missiles (and take the scimitar/claw hits). Either way you win if you read it correctly.

vs. Med-Level — Resource War

V
Learning Death — Claws at Wrong Time
Defeat
Classic early spec mistake.
You Dragon Claws spec at 75 HP opponent with 8 food. First hit: 20. Total: 20+10+5+5 = 40.
They combo eat through 40 damage — back to 75 HP. Your spec bar: 50% gone.
Fight grinds. You run low on food first at 1 Defence.
Opponent Dragon Claws at 40 HP you: 28+14+7+7 = 56. Dead.
Lesson: A 20 first-hit Claws spec against a full-HP stocked opponent was the mistake. They ate through 40 total damage trivially. The same Claws spec when they're at 55 HP with 1 food left would have killed cleanly. First-hit matters — but timing matters more.

Tips & Mistakes

  • 01
    Never spec above 55 HP opponent with food. Dragon Claws with a 20 first-hit deals 40 total — they eat through it. Wait for 55 HP or below with 1–2 food left.
  • 02
    Anglerfish overheal before every fight. At 1 Defence, a lucky 35-hit from any spec weapon is potentially lethal from base HP. Overheal is your safety margin.
  • 03
    Pot both Super Attack AND Super Strength. Super Strength raises max hit. Super Attack raises accuracy. At 1 Defence where every fight is a race, both matter.
  • 04
    MSB (i) is not optional. Any opponent who prays Melee throughout the fight completely shuts down your scimitar and Claws. The MSB prayer bypass is your answer.
  • 05
    Count their food. You're waiting for the food depletion window to spec. If you can see their inventory (BH UI, or watching them eat), count down. Spec when they're on last food.
  • 06
    Dragon Claws first hit decides everything. A 30+ first hit means 60+ total. A 15 first hit means 30 total. Pre-eat if their Claws bar is at 50% — anticipate the spec and eat to stay out of kill range.
  • 07
    Never train Defence. 1-Defence is the entire premise of the build. Any XP is permanent. Check every quest reward, every training method, every XP lamp.
The 60 Attack Pure is arguably the best all-round 1-Defence build — you have the fast scimitar for grinding, the Dragon Claws for closing, and the option of adding MSB for prayer bypass. The only thing missing compared to a zerker is the defence level.

Where to Fight

Clan Wars

Drill Dragon Claws F1 equip + spec, MSB prayer reading switch, and combo eating. The Claws spec needs to be instant under pressure.

Bounty Hunter

Primary competitive environment. Matched targets. BH points imbue your Berserker Ring (i). Best bracket for this build.

Edgeville Wilderness

Other pures, zerkers, and mid-level accounts regularly appear here. Rich target pool at combat 78–82. Stay level 1–10 initially.

Last Man Standing

No gear advantage here but excellent for Dragon Claws timing and MSB switching mechanics.

At combat 80, you occasionally face 90–100 CB accounts. Always assess their gear — Bandos + Torture is manageable, Scythe/TBow is not your fight.
