The Range Tank

High Defence, 99 Ranged — absorb melee hits while firing Blowpipe or TBow

The Range Tank is a high-Defence account built around 99 Ranged. Unlike the Pure Ranger with 1 Defence, the Range Tank has 70–99 Defence — meaning opponents with melee weapons deal significantly reduced damage while your Blowpipe or Twisted Bow fires at full efficiency. You combine the offensive power of top-tier ranged with the defensive durability of a maxed defence account.

Range Tanks are most effective in situations where opponents expect a ranged skirmish but find their melee switches are nearly useless. A Twisted Bow Range Tank at combat 110–120 is one of the most durable hybrid opponents in the game — constantly dealing ranged damage while being nearly immune to melee hits.

A Range Tank with 99 Defence reduces a Dragon Scimitar hit from ~30 average to ~12 average. Combined with Protect from Missiles when opponents switch to ranged, and you're effectively untouchable by most builds below 120 combat.

Fight Examples

I
vs. Melee Pure — Untouchable
Victory
Pure (99 Str, 60 Atk, 1 Def) vs Range Tank (99 Ranged, 99 Def, TBow).
ScimPure engages. Dragon Scimitar hits reduced to 6, 9, 4, 11 by 99 Defence.
TBow fires: 44, 38, 51. Pure takes full damage — 1 Defence.
Pure eaten through 6 sharks. Can't deal meaningful damage.
TBow: 47. Kill. Range Tank never needed to eat.
Lesson: The matchup was completely asymmetric. The pure's Dragon Scimitar averaging 7.5 damage vs 99 Defence is negligible. The TBow's 44-damage average vs 1 Defence is overwhelming. The Range Tank barely registered the melee contact.
II
vs. Zerker — More Even Fight
Victory
Zerker (45 Def, Dragon Claws, 88 CB) vs Range Tank (110 CB). Higher CB gap.
Zerker opens with MSB spec: 19+23. Range Tank at 79 HP. Eats once.
TBow fires: 38, 44. Zerker eating.
Zerker Dragon Claws: 28+14+7+7 = 56. Range Tank at 45 HP. Combo eat.
TBow: 41, 36. Zerker dead.
Lesson: The zerker's specs landed but the Range Tank's much higher HP (99 base) and 99 Defence absorbed the burst. TBow's consistent high damage overwhelmed the zerker's food before their second spec could land.

Tips

  • 01
    Your Defence is your primary weapon. A Range Tank wins by outlasting opponents. You absorb their melee hits trivially while TBow/Blowpipe deals full damage. Never abandon this advantage by trying to melee fight.
  • 02
    Switch to Protect from Missiles immediately when opponents go ranged. Your high Defence only protects against melee. Ranged attacks still bypass it. Protect Missiles + melee switch is your defensive response.
  • 03
    Twisted Bow scales with opponent Magic level. Against mage pures, void hybrids, and endgame accounts with high magic, TBow deals devastating damage. Know your matchups.
  • 04
    This is a high-investment build. Masori armour, TBow, Anguish, Assembler — total investment is 2–3 billion GP. Make sure the build appeals to your playstyle before committing.
  • 05
    Combat level will be high (110–120). You're not hiding at low CB. This build is for players who want to fight high-CB opponents on even terms with a defensive advantage.
