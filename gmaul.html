The G Maul Pure

60 Attack, 1 Defence, 99 Strength — maximum burst at low combat

The G Maul Pure is the most refined entry point into burst-spec PKing. Unlike the Obby Mauler that uses no Attack at all, this build trains Attack to 60 specifically to unlock the Dragon Scimitar — giving you a fast, accurate auto-attack weapon to chip opponents down before the Granite Maul double spec finishes them.

The core loop is simple: grind with the scimitar, watch the opponent's food supply, and fire both G Maul specs the moment they have 2 or fewer food items left. The 0-tick spec mechanic means both hits arrive before any eating response is possible.

Combat Level Breakdown

With 60 Attack, 99 Strength, 1 Defence, and ~70 HP: combat level sits around 78–82 depending on Prayer. This bracket is rich with other pures, low-level mains, and zerker accounts — all excellent matchups for the G Maul finish.

Prayer choices: 1 Prayer keeps combat lowest (~78 CB). 31 Prayer unlocks Protect from Melee (+3 CB). 44 Prayer covers all three Protect prayers (+4 CB). 52 Prayer adds Smite (+5 CB). Most G Maul pures run 44–52 Prayer.

G Maul Spec — Reminder

Always carry the Granite Maul in your inventory. On the closing tick, equip it from inventory then right-click → Use Special Attack twice in rapid succession. Both specs arrive within 1–2 ticks — faster than any opponent can eat.

Tips & Mistakes

What separates a clean G Maul Pure from an expensive food dispenser

  • 01
    Always overheal with Anglerfish. At 1 Defence, any hit above 50 HP is potentially fatal. Starting at 92 HP instead of 70 HP is the margin that keeps you alive during the grind phase.
  • 02
    Drink Super Attack before every fight. More accuracy means more scimitar hits landing, which means faster food depletion from the opponent, which means a better spec window.
  • 03
    Never spec above 50 HP opponent with food. They will combo eat through it and your spec bar is empty. The window is sub-50 HP with 1–2 food left.
  • 04
    Carry MSB (i) for prayer counters. Any opponent who activates Protect from Melee completely shuts down your scimitar and G Maul. The MSB spec bypasses prayer.
  • 05
    The G Maul spec is right-click only. Left-clicking equips but does not spec. Right-click the weapon → Use Special Attack. Drill this until it's instant muscle memory.
  • 06
    Escape after failed specs. No spec bar + opponent with food = a fight you will lose at 1 Defence. Stamina Potion and run.
  • 07
    Never train Defence. Any Defence XP permanently raises your combat level, pushing you into tougher brackets without any defensive benefit at the level gains involved.
Training note: Train Strength at NMZ for minimal HP gain per Strength level. High HP raises your combat level, putting you in brackets where opponents have proportionally more HP and gear.

Where to Fight

Best hunting grounds for the G Maul Pure at combat 78–82

Clan Wars

Practice the G Maul right-click double spec, scimitar timing, and combo eating here first. No risk. Drill until automatic.

Bounty Hunter

Matched targets near your combat level. Excellent bracket — other pures and low-level accounts. BH points imbue your Berserker Ring.

Edgeville Wilderness (Levels 1–10)

Primary hunting ground. Other pures, F2P accounts visiting members worlds, and low-level mains. Close to Edgeville bank for fast restocking.

Last Man Standing

Standardised gear removes your Obsidian/Necklace advantage, but the scimitar + G Maul spec timing practice is identical. Good mechanical drilling environment.

The G Maul Pure performs best against opponents whose HP and food supply is proportional to the combat bracket (~70–80 HP, 6–8 food). Against heavily stocked opponents, the scimitar grind takes too long at 1 Defence. Target selection is everything.
