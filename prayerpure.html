The Prayer Pure

Maximise offensive prayer bonuses while keeping combat level minimal

The Prayer Pure is any low-Defence account that specifically invests in Prayer levels for the offensive and defensive bonuses they provide — going beyond the minimum 31–44 Prayer most pures stop at. The defining feature is leveraging prayers like Piety (70 Prayer), Smite (52 Prayer), and Rigour (74 Prayer) to dramatically boost offensive output at a combat level far lower than a defence account would need to be.

A 60 Attack pure with 70 Prayer (Piety) and 1 Defence at combat ~88 hits with the same melee multipliers as a full-defence account with Piety, but fights opponents who have significantly less HP and gear. The asymmetry is the strategy.

Piety adds +23% Strength and +20% Attack to your combat. At 99 Strength, this is a massive DPS increase. A 1-Defence pure with Piety at combat 85 deals more damage per tick than most combat 100 accounts without prayer.

The Piety Decision

Adding Piety to a 60 Attack pure costs about 8–9 combat levels but increases melee DPS by roughly 23%. Whether this is worth it depends on your target bracket — in some brackets those 8–9 CB levels push you into significantly tougher fights that negate the DPS gain. In higher brackets where the opponents are already tough, Piety is clearly worth it.

Tips

  • 01
    Piety is the highest-impact prayer investment. At 70 Prayer, +23% Strength is a bigger DPS multiplier than most gear upgrades at this combat level. If you're going to invest in Prayer beyond 44, go to 70.
  • 02
    Monitor prayer points actively in every fight. Piety drains fast. Sip at 20 prayer points — not at 5 or 0. Running out of Piety mid-fight costs you the DPS advantage you paid combat levels for.
  • 03
    Smite and Piety serve different purposes. Smite is used against prayer-reliant opponents to drain their prayer. Piety is used to boost your own DPS. Swap between them based on the fight — don't run Piety against a prayer tank (they block your attacks regardless), run Smite.
  • 04
    The Holy Wrench increases prayer restoration. Carrying it in your inventory means each Prayer Potion dose restores 2 extra prayer points at 70+ Prayer. Minor but cumulative over a long trip.
  • 05
    King's Ransom is required for Piety. The quest has no Defence XP reward and is safe for all builds. Do it before grinding to 70 Prayer.
