Mage Tank

High Defence + 99 Magic — tank melee while dealing magic damage freely

The Mage Tank builds high Defence (70–99) alongside 99 Magic. Unlike most magic builds that operate at low Defence, the Mage Tank intentionally trains Defence to near-maximum — making melee hits negligible while firing Ice Barrage, Trident of the Swamp, or Vengeance-stacked spells freely.

The core asymmetry: at 99 Defence, melee attacks from pures and zerkers hit for 3–8 average damage per hit. Your magic spells through Ancestral robes deal 20–30 per cast. You're winning an attritional fight where they deal 6 while you deal 25.

Fight Examples

I
vs. Pure — Attrition Win
Victory
Mage Tank (110 CB, 99 Def) vs ScimPure (1 Def, 80 CB, Dragon Scimitar).
ScimPure scimitar hits: 6, 9, 4, 11, 7. Average 7.4 over 5 hits.
Your Ice Barrage: 24. Freeze. Blood Barrage: 20, 18, 22.
ScimPure has eaten 4 sharks. You've taken 37 damage total — haven't eaten once.
Re-freeze. More casts. Kill. You never needed to eat.
Lesson: The attrition was completely one-sided. Their scimitar hit for 7.4 average vs your 99 Defence — you took 37 damage in a 12-tick fight and never ate. Your Barrage dealt 84 damage in the same time. The food economy was irrelevant because you simply didn't need food.
II
vs. Ranged TBow — The Hard Matchup
Escaped
Range Tank with TBow vs Mage Tank. TBow bypasses your defence.
TBow fires: 52. Your 99 Defence does nothing — TBow is ranged.
Activate Protect Missiles. TBow blocked. Switch to melee.
Extended fight — TBow blocked, but their melee also hits hard at 126 CB.
Food running low. Escape with Stamina Potion.
Escaped. Learned: prepare a Protect Missiles switch for TBow opponents.
Lesson: The Mage Tank's weakness is ranged — 99 Defence doesn't reduce ranged damage. Protect from Missiles is essential when opponents switch to TBow or Blowpipe. Always carry the prayer points for this switch.

Tips

  • 01
    99 Defence doesn't protect against ranged or magic. Only melee attacks are reduced by Defence. Protect from Missiles when opponents go ranged.
  • 02
    Ancestral provides magic damage bonus — Ahrim's doesn't. Ancestral robes have no Defence requirement, only 75 Magic. Always use Ancestral for offensive magic builds.
  • 03
    Ice Barrage freeze is still your best opener. Even against melee pures whose hits are negligible, the freeze prevents escape and forces them to take full magic damage while locked in place.
  • 04
    This build is expensive. Ancestral set, Kodai Wand, Imbued God Cape — significant GP investment. Consider whether you prefer Range Tank (rangetank.html) or Mage Tank based on your GP and playstyle.
  • 05
    The Occult Necklace is non-negotiable. +10% magic damage on every cast. At 99 Magic with Ice Barrage, this is the single highest-impact amulet slot item.
