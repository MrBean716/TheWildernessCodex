The Main Account

No restrictions — balanced stats, flexible gear, fights everywhere

A main account in OSRS PvP has no artificially capped stats. You train Attack, Strength, Defence, Ranged, Magic, and Prayer to whatever level serves your goals, and you fight using whatever gear you can afford. Mains are the most flexible PvP accounts — you can switch between melee, ranged, and magic freely, wear any armour, and access every quest reward.

The trade-off is combat level. Because mains train Defence and all offensive stats, their combat level rises proportionally. At 75/99/75 a main sits around combat 104. At 90/99/90 around 117. Every stat level adds CB, which changes the bracket you fight in and the opponents you encounter.

Mains don't have a single 'build' — they have a gear and stat progression. This guide covers the 70–100 CB range where most non-pure mains operate before reaching the endgame 104–126 bracket.

Fight Examples

I
vs. 1-Def Pure — Defence Advantage
Victory
You (main, 85 CB, Bandos, 75 Def) vs ScimPure (pure, 80 CB, 1 Def).
Pure's scimitar hits reduced by your 75 Defence to ~12 average.
Your whip hits through their 1 Defence: 29, 33, 27.
They eat 6 sharks. You've eaten 1.
Dragon Claws: 31+15+7+7 = 60. Kill.
Lesson: Your 75 Defence halved their effective DPS. The food economy was never close — they ate 6 times to your 1. Mains beat pures through Defence reducing their damage, not through superior offence.
II
vs. Another Main — Gear Decides
Victory
You (Bandos, Whip, AGS) vs GearMain (Rune armour, Dragon Scimitar).
Your Bandos significantly outgears their rune. Whip accuracy dominates.
They DDS spec: 18+14. You combo eat.
AGS spec from 60 HP: 58. Kill.
Lesson: Between mains, gear is the primary deciding factor before mechanics even come into play. Bandos vs Rune is not a close fight — the Strength bonus difference is enormous.

Tips

  • 01
    Mains improve linearly with stats and gear. Unlike pures where combat level is carefully managed, a main simply gets stronger as you train and upgrade. Prioritise: 99 Strength → Piety (70 Prayer) → Whip (75 Atk) → Bandos.
  • 02
    Protect from Melee is your best defensive tool. At most CB levels you'll face, their primary damage style is melee. Protect Melee halves your food consumption in many fights.
  • 03
    Don't neglect Ranged. A main with 80+ Ranged and a Blowpipe switch is significantly more threatening than a melee-only main. Blowpipe bypass forces prayer switching.
  • 04
    Vengeance (94 Magic) is the single biggest late-game upgrade. The Veng+spec combo is how mains punch above their weight. Add it once your base combat stats are solid.
  • 05
    Super Combat Potion replaces three separate pots. Attack + Strength + Defence in one dose. Always use Super Combat over individual pots.
