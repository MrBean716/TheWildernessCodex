Baby Pure

Sub-50 Combat — maximum asymmetry, minimum investment

A Baby Pure is an account intentionally kept at very low combat level — typically sub-50 CB — while training Strength high enough to overpower opponents in that bracket. The target audience is F2P worlds and low-level Wilderness where opponents have 20–35 HP and minimal food.

Baby Pures are often F2P accounts or accounts early in their progression. The strategy is pure asymmetry: your combat level looks harmless, your Strength hits like a much higher-level account. Opponents in the sub-50 bracket frequently carry 0–3 food items and have no protection prayers.

Rotations

At sub-50 CB most fights end in 2–4 ticks. Your double spec arrives before opponents can process what's happening — they're dead before their eat animation resolves.

Tips

  • 01
    Target low-HP, low-food opponents exclusively. A Baby Pure loses extended fights. Your entire strategy is killing before food becomes relevant.
  • 02
    F2P worlds are your natural hunting ground. F2P food (Lobsters, Tuna, Swordfish) heals less than P2P food. F2P players also rarely have prayer and carry fewer supplies.
  • 03
    Super Strength Potion before every fight. At lower Strength levels the potion adds proportionally more to your max hit than at 99 Str. Never skip it.
  • 04
    Build toward 99 Strength — the Baby Pure is temporary. Once your Strength is 99 consider transitioning to a proper Obby Mauler or G Maul Pure for better long-term effectiveness.
