The Tribrid Main

126 Combat — Melee, Ranged, and Magic simultaneously in a single fight

The Tribrid Main is the apex predator of OSRS PvP. At 126 combat with 99 in Attack, Strength, Defence, Ranged, and Magic, you use all three combat styles in a single fight — switching between Scythe of Vitur, Twisted Bow, and Ice Barrage within seconds to ensure your opponent can never find a protection prayer that blocks all your damage.

Tribriding is the endgame of PvP skill expression in OSRS. You're managing Vengeance cooldowns, prayer switching, style switching, eating timing, and spec bar management — all simultaneously in 0.6-second tick windows. The reward is a combat style so difficult to defend against that opponents with equivalent gear can be defeated by superior execution alone.

A fully executed tribrid sequence (freeze → melee burst while frozen → TBow as they pray Melee → Veng+AGS close) can deal 150+ damage in 4–5 ticks. No combination of protection prayers blocks all three styles simultaneously.

I
Full Tribrid Sequence — The Ideal Fight
Victory
You (126 CB, full tribrid) vs MaxedOpponent (126 CB, full gear). Execution decides it.
Approach. Veng pre-cast. Overheal to 121 HP.
Ice Barrage freeze: 28 magic damage. Opponent frozen.
Immediately switch to Torva + Scythe. Piety. Scythe: 44+29+18 = 91 while frozen.
Opponent unfreezes. Activates Protect Melee. Switch to TBow.

Lesson: The freeze converted 5 free ticks of Scythe hits into 91 damage they couldn't dodge. The TBow bypassed their Protect Melee. The re-freeze prevented escape. The Veng+AGS combo closed it. Every style served a specific function — none was wasted.

Tips

  • 01
    Tribrid switching must be automatic before live use. Five-to-six item swaps per style change in 0.6 seconds requires thousands of repetitions. Clan Wars tribriding practice — for months — before real fights.
  • 02
    The freeze opener is your most powerful move. Ice Barrage freeze plus immediate Scythe switch deals 90+ damage in 5 ticks before opponents can respond. Pre-positioning for the freeze is critical.
  • 03
    Vengeance timing is everything. Pre-cast during approach. Re-cast after triggering. The Veng+AGS close frequently deals 80–100+ combined damage — often the fight-ending move.
  • 04
    Investment is enormous. Scythe of Vitur, Twisted Bow, Ancestral set, Masori set, Torva set, Kodai Wand — total investment is 3–6 billion GP. Ensure you enjoy this playstyle before committing.
  • 05
    You will lose to better tribriders. The skill ceiling of tribriding is extremely high. Accept defeats as learning opportunities. Review fights, identify mistakes, improve one mechanic at a time.
  • 06
    Prayer management is the hidden complexity. Switching between Piety (melee), Rigour (ranged), and Augury (magic) while also activating protection prayers against the opponent's style — all while eating and speccing — is the true depth of tribriding.
