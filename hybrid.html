The Hybrid Pure

1 Defence, multi-style combat — fight with whichever style bypasses their active prayer

A Hybrid Pure is a 1-Defence account that trains multiple combat styles — typically Melee and Ranged, sometimes adding Magic — and switches between them mid-fight based on the opponent's protection prayer. Where a melee pure is completely shut down by Protect from Melee, a hybrid simply switches style and continues dealing damage.

The hybrid concept is the natural evolution of the 1-Defence pure. You take the same 99 Strength foundation as a melee pure, add 99 Ranged for a Toxic Blowpipe and MSB spec, and gain the ability to attack from any prayer state. Your opponents can no longer simply activate one protection prayer and wait you out.

The most important hybrid mechanic: see their protection prayer, switch to the style it doesn't protect against. Protect Melee active → Blowpipe. Protect Missiles active → Dragon Scimitar. No prayer → attack with whichever style is highest DPS.

Build Variants

Two-style vs three-style hybrid configuration

Two-Style Hybrid (Melee + Ranged)

The most common hybrid configuration. Train Attack to 60 (Dragon Scimitar) or 75 (Abyssal Whip), 99 Strength, and 99 Ranged. This gives you the Blowpipe for ranged style and the scimitar/whip + Dragon Claws or AGS for melee finishing.

Three-Style Tribrid (Melee + Ranged + Magic)

Adds a magic switch (typically Ice Rush/Burst for freezing) to the two-style hybrid. The freeze prevents opponent movement, letting you land melee hits on an immobile target or burst them while frozen. The highest skill demand of any 1-Defence build.

The tribrid freeze opener: cast Ice Burst to freeze, then switch to melee weapon and hit freely while they're frozen — they prayed Protect Magic thinking you'd keep casting, but now your melee bypasses it.

Fight Examples

Five fights showing hybrid style switching in practice

I
vs. 1-Def Melee Pure — Prayer Bypass Dominates
Victory
Opponent (60 Atk pure, combat 80) activates Protect Melee at fight start.
PureAtk activates Protect Melee. Scimitar would deal 0.
You switch to Blowpipe — unblocked: 24, 28, 22.
PureAtk switches to Protect Missiles. You switch back to Scimitar.
Scimitar: 31, 26. They've eaten most of their food.
Dragon Claws: 32+16+8+8 = 64. Kill from 55 HP.
Lesson: Their Protect Melee was completely irrelevant — you switched to Blowpipe and dealt 74 unprotected ranged damage while they thought prayer was keeping them safe. When they switched to Protect Missiles, you'd already won the food economy.
II
vs. Zerker — Outmanoeuvring
Victory
Zerker (45 Def, Dragon Claws, combat 90) vs Hybrid Pure (1 Def, combat 88).
You MSB (i) opener before prayer activates: 19+25 = 44.
Zerker activates Protect Missiles. You switch to Scimitar.
Scimitar exchange — their 45 Def reduces but you still land 22, 18, 27.
Zerker switches to Protect Melee. You switch to Blowpipe.
Blowpipe: 23, 19. Their food is almost gone.
Dragon Claws through Protect Missiles: 27+13+6+6 = 52. Kill from 48 HP.
Lesson: The continuous style switching meant the zerker's prayers never provided sustained protection. Every time they activated a prayer, you'd already switched to bypass it. The MSB opener dealt 44 pre-prayer damage — that food debt was never recovered from.
III
vs. Void Pure — The Hardest Match
Victory
Void Pure is also a hybrid — both switching styles simultaneously.
Both players switch styles rapidly. Void Pure opens Blowpipe.
You Protect Missiles. They switch to Void Melee Helm.
You switch to Blowpipe through their Protect Missiles.
Rapid prayer exchanges — fight is determined by reaction speed.
You Veng triggers on their hit. AGS: 57. Veng recoil: 18. 75 burst. Kill from 62 HP.
Lesson: Void Pure mirrors come down to who reacts 1 tick faster. Your Veng+AGS combo was the fight-deciding factor — the same burst combo used by zerkers and 75 Attack pures. Having Vengeance (94 Magic) elevates the Hybrid Pure significantly.
IV
vs. Med-Level — Outpunching With Style
Victory
Med-Level (75/99/75, Bandos, combat 104) fights a Hybrid Pure (combat 88, 1 Def).
MSB opener — 22+18 = 40 before prayer.
BandosMed activates Protect Missiles. You Scimitar.
Trade scimitar vs whip. Their 75 Def reduces you but 99 Str still lands hits.
They DDS spec. You survive, combo eat.
Blowpipe switch when they activate Protect Melee: 26, 21, 24.
Dragon Claws: 29+14+7+7 = 57. Kill from 52 HP.
Lesson: The hybrid approach forced BandosMed to chase the 'correct' prayer while you simply switched styles. They never had a prayer state that blocked all your damage — you always had an unblocked option.
V
Learning Death — Late Style Switch
Defeat
Hesitated on the style switch — kept firing Blowpipe through Protect Missiles.
Opponent activates Protect Missiles.
You keep firing Blowpipe — all hits: 0, 0, 0, 0. Six wasted ticks.
During those wasted ticks their scimitar hit you for 22, 19, 28. You're at 23 HP.
Too late to recover the food gap. Dead.
Lesson: The entire value of hybridding is the style switch. Firing through a protection prayer deals literally zero damage — you're attacking for free while they hit you for full damage. The rule is absolute: see the prayer, switch the style immediately. Every tick of delay is free HP for them and wasted resources for you.

Tips & Mistakes

  • 01
    Switch styles the instant you see a protection prayer. No delay. See the prayer icon → switch weapon → continue attacking. Any hesitation is free damage for them and zero damage from you.
  • 02
    Overheal with Anglerfish every fight. Hybrid fights run longer than pure melee fights because opponents may prayer-switch actively. More hits means more damage taken. Those extra HP are essential.
  • 03
    The MSB opener is your most powerful move. Most opponents activate Protect Melee at fight start — your MSB spec fires before prayer activates. 35–45 pre-prayer damage is a fight-defining head start.
  • 04
    Practice style switches in Clan Wars until completely automatic. In a real fight you have 0.6 seconds to switch. The muscle memory needs to be perfect — see Protect Melee, hands switch to Blowpipe without any conscious thought.
  • 05
    Bring both spec weapons. Dragon Claws closes melee fights; MSB (i) closes ranged fights. Having only one spec weapon means you can't close optimally against certain prayer states.
  • 06
    Vengeance (94 Magic) dramatically elevates this build. Adding Veng to a hybrid pure's toolkit creates Veng+spec burst capable of 80–90 damage in one tick — the same finishing power as a full zerker or 75 Attack pure.
  • 07
    Count their food. The hybrid style switching creates multiple damage streams — it's easy to lose track of fight state. Occasionally assess: how much food do they have left? You're trying to create a kill window, not just swap styles endlessly.
The Hybrid Pure is the most demanding 1-Defence build after the tribrid. It rewards players who've already mastered a single-style pure and want to add the prayer-bypass layer. If you're new to PvP, start with the 1-Def Pure guide and the 60 Attack Pure first.

Where to Fight

Clan Wars

Drill style switches until they require zero conscious thought. The 1-tick window to switch is unforgiving — this mechanic needs to be completely automatic before live fighting.

Bounty Hunter

The hybrid build absolutely thrives here. Matched targets, consistent bracket, and BH UI shows opponent inventory — helping you count food and time your spec window.

Edgeville Wilderness

Varied opponents — other pures, zerkers, mid-levels. The hybrid style switching is effective against the widest possible range of builds you'll encounter here.

Last Man Standing

Standardised gear removes your weapon advantage, but the prayer-reading and style-switching practice is directly applicable. Excellent for developing the reaction speed this build requires.

When you're comfortable as a Hybrid Pure, the step up to Void Pure becomes much more manageable — void pures use identical style-switching mechanics but with the added Void set bonus and single-helm swap speed advantage.
