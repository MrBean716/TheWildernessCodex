75 Attack Zerker

45 Defence, 75 Attack — Abyssal Whip accuracy meets AGS burst on a zerker frame

The 75 Attack Zerker pushes the zerker build to its offensive ceiling by training Attack to 75 for the Abyssal Whip. The Whip's +82 Slash accuracy is dramatically higher than the Dragon Scimitar's +67 — meaning significantly more hits land during the grind phase and the spec window opens faster. Combined with the Armadyl Godsword (also 75 Atk req) and Vengeance, this is the highest-DPS zerker configuration.

The trade-off is combat level. At 45 Def, 75 Atk, 99 Str, your combat sits around 95–100 — above the standard 60 Atk zerker bracket (~90). You'll face stronger opponents, but your significantly better accuracy and spec options compensate.

Tips

  • 01
    Veng+AGS is the defining combo of this build. The 75 Attack Zerker's signature play. Pre-cast Veng, let their hit land, fire AGS immediately. 80–100+ burst damage in one tick.
  • 02
    MSB (i) opener before prayer activates is devastating. Most opponents activate Protect Melee at fight start. Your MSB spec fires before they react — 35–46 unprotected ranged damage as an opener.
  • 03
    Piety (70 Prayer) is worth training to. At 75 Attack + Whip, Piety's +20% Attack and +23% Strength stack multiplicatively to produce noticeably higher DPS than without. The CB cost is real but justified.
  • 04
    The AGS spec window is tighter than Dragon Claws. AGS at 75 HP with full food will be eaten through. Wait for 55–65 HP with last food gone. The spec is boom-bust — time it correctly.
  • 05
    45 Defence is still your Achilles heel. Opponent specs hit harder than they would against a 75 Def account. Anglerfish overheal is essential — those extra HP absorb the burst that would otherwise kill you.
