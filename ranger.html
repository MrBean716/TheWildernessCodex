The Pure Ranger

1 Defence, 99 Ranged — pure ranged DPS with spec-weapon finishes

The Pure Ranger is a 1-Defence account built entirely around Ranged combat. At 99 Ranged with a Toxic Blowpipe, you fire every 3 ticks — faster than any melee weapon at the same combat level. The Blowpipe's rapid hits chip opponents down while the Magic Shortbow (i) special attack delivers two simultaneous arrows as a spec finish or prayer bypass.

The Pure Ranger's primary advantage is mobility: ranged weapons can fire while moving, allowing you to kite melee-focused opponents, maintain distance from threatening specs, and fire while escaping. It's a fundamentally different combat style to melee pures — you choose the range of engagement, not just the timing.

Unlike melee pures that must close to attack, a Pure Ranger can deal damage while running. Against a melee pure trying to close the gap, you can fire 3–5 Blowpipe shots while they're still sprinting toward you.

Rotations

Kite, chip, and close with MSB spec

Phase 1

Opener — Start at Distance

Ranged opener
Anglerfish overheal→Ranging Potion + Rigour→Maintain distance→Blowpipe fires every 3 ticks
Phase 2

Kiting vs Melee Opponents

Kite mechanic
Melee opponent approaches→Move 1 tile per tick→Blowpipe fires while moving→They can't reach melee range→Chip damage accumulates
The 1-tile kite: move one tile away, fire, move one tile, fire. This keeps you just outside melee range while landing Blowpipe shots. Not all opponents can be kited — prayer users and ranged opponents require different tactics.
Phase 3

MSB Spec Close

MSB finish
Opponent low HP, food low→Equip MSB (i)→F1 → Spec→2 arrows fire simultaneously→Back to Blowpipe if needed

Fight Examples

I
vs. 1-Def Melee Pure — Kiting Works
Victory
You (Pure Ranger, 99 Ranged, combat 82) vs ScimPure (99 Str, 60 Atk, 1 Def, combat 80).
ScimPure charges. You activate Rigour, pot up, start firing Blowpipe while running.
You fire 18, 24, 21 while kiting — they can't reach melee range.
ScimPure activates Protect Missiles. You move into melee range — now their scimitar can reach but your Blowpipe still fires.
Trade hits. You combo eat at 35 HP.
MSB spec when they're low: 23+27 = 50. Kill.
Lesson: The kiting phase dealt ~63 damage before any melee contact. When they prayed Missiles you closed range — neither of you had a prayer advantage at melee range, but you'd already won the food economy.
II
vs. Zerker — Ranged Through Protect Melee
Victory
Zerker activates Protect Melee at fight start — your Blowpipe doesn't care.
ZerkFighter activates Protect Melee immediately.
You Blowpipe fires unblocked: 26, 22, 19. Through Protect Melee entirely.
ZerkFighter switches to Protect Missiles. You close to melee range but Blowpipe still reaches.
Dragon Claws spec: 24+12+6+6 = 48. You combo eat.
MSB spec: 19+28 = 47 from 52 HP. Kill.
Lesson: Ranged completely bypasses Protect Melee. The zerker's primary prayer did nothing. When they switched to Protect Missiles, you'd already dealt 67 Blowpipe damage — the food deficit was insurmountable.
III
vs. Another Ranger — Prayer Race
Victory
Mirror match — both have Blowpipes and Rigour.
Both fire Blowpipes. Even DPS exchange.
Opponent activates Protect Missiles — your Blowpipe deals 0.
You immediately switch to melee style (Dragon Scimitar if carrying one) or accept and close range.
Extended fight — whoever manages prayers and food better wins.
You MSB spec when opponent is low food: 31+24. Kill.
Lesson: Ranger mirrors demand prayer reading. The moment they activate Protect Missiles, firing is wasted. Having a melee switch (even a cheap one) forces them into an impossible prayer situation.
IV
vs. Mage Pure — Close Range is Key
Victory
Pure Mage opponent — spells hit hard but you can close the distance.
MagePure activates Protect Magic before engaging. Spells fire regardless.
You close the gap immediately — at melee range, casting spells is harder and you can fire Blowpipe.
Blowpipe chips through their cloth armour: 28, 31, 25.
MagePure tries Barrage freeze. You stay mobile.
MSB spec: 22+29 = 51. Kill from 44 HP.
Lesson: Closing to melee range against a Mage Pure removes their safe-casting distance. Your cloth-punching Blowpipe tears through their low Defence rapidly. Moving constantly reduces freeze landing probability.
V
Learning Death — Stopped Moving
Defeat
Stopped kiting — gave up the Pure Ranger's main advantage.
ScimPure approaches. You stop moving to click food at 40 HP.
They close the gap in the 2 ticks you stood still. Scimitar: 33.
You try to eat and fire — scimitar lands again: 28. Dead from 40 HP.
Lesson: Stopping movement to eat let them close the distance and remove your kiting advantage. On a Pure Ranger, eat ON THE MOVE — click food and immediately continue moving. Your survival depends on maintaining distance.

Tips & Mistakes

  • 01
    Never stop moving unnecessarily. Every tick you stand still is a tick a melee opponent closes distance. Eat while moving. Always move.
  • 02
    Blowpipe scales are limited — check before every trip. Running out of scales mid-fight removes your primary weapon. 500+ scales per trip minimum.
  • 03
    Ranging Potion every fight, re-sip every 60 seconds. Ranged level directly affects both accuracy and max hit. A depleted Ranging Potion is equivalent to losing 10+ Ranged levels mid-fight.
  • 04
    Protect Missiles from opponents is your hardest counter. Against sustained Protect Missiles, you deal 0 damage. Having a melee weapon as a switch (Dragon Scimitar) forces them into an impossible prayer situation.
  • 05
    Rigour (74 Prayer) makes an enormous difference. +23% ranged damage and +20% accuracy from Rigour is a massive DPS increase. If you have 74 Prayer, Rigour should be active in every fight.
  • 06
    Kite aggressively against melee opponents. Melee can't hit you at range. Every tick you kite safely is a free Blowpipe hit. Most melee pures at this combat level will never catch a focused kiter.

Where to Fight

Bounty Hunter

Best bracket — matched targets. Pure Ranger is very effective here because prayer coverage at low combat levels is inconsistent among your targets.

Edgeville Wilderness

Open terrain for kiting. Level 1–10 Wilderness gives you space to manoeuvre and an Edgeville bank for fast restock. Kiting melee opponents in this zone is highly effective.

Clan Wars

Practice the kite mechanic extensively here. Learn exactly how many tiles you need to maintain to stay out of melee range while landing Blowpipe shots.

The Pure Ranger is one of the hardest builds to escape from as a melee player — you can deal damage while running. Take advantage of this both offensively (kiting) and defensively (firing while escaping).
