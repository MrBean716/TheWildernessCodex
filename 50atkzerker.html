50 Attack Zerker

45 Defence, 50 Attack — the pre-Dragon-Scimitar zerker bracket

The 50 Attack Zerker stops Attack at exactly 50 — below the Dragon Scimitar threshold of 60. This is a deliberate choice: at 50 Attack you stay at a lower combat level than a 60 Attack zerker while still accessing the Dragon Longsword (60 Atk req — wait, actually Dragon Longsword requires 60 Attack).

Correction: The Dragon Longsword requires 60 Attack, not 50. At 50 Attack the best melee weapons are the Rune Scimitar (40 Atk) and the Rune Sword (40 Atk). The 50 Attack bracket exists primarily because it keeps combat level lower while training Strength to 99 — you fight in the same bracket as 1-Def pures but with 45 Defence. At exactly 50 Attack you can wield the Dragon Scimitar's stat inferior but the bracket is the draw.

The honest appeal of 50 Attack: it's an intermediate zerker bracket. At 50 Attack, 99 Strength, 45 Defence, your combat level (~88–92) is slightly lower than a 60 Attack zerker (~92–96), letting you fight opponents in a slightly weaker pool. The primary weapon is the Rune Scimitar (40 Atk) with the Granite Maul as the spec closer.

Fight Examples

I
vs. 1-Def Pure — 45 Def Tanking
Victory
You (50 Atk Zerker, 45 Def, ~88 CB) vs ScimPure (1 Def, 60 Atk, ~80 CB).
Their Dragon Scim partially absorbed by your 45 Def: ~18 average.
Your Rune Scim at 99 Str through their 1 Def: 25, 28, 22.
G Maul double spec from 43 HP: 34 + 27 = 61. Kill.
Lesson: At 99 Strength the Rune Scimitar hits harder than many players expect — 99 Str compensates for the lower accuracy significantly. The G Maul close is identical to the base Obby Mauler spec sequence.

Tips

  • 01
    99 Strength largely compensates for the Rune Scimitar's lower accuracy. At 99 Str you hit so hard that the accuracy gap vs Dragon Scimitar matters less than players expect.
  • 02
    The G Maul spec is your great equaliser. Regardless of Attack level, the G Maul 0-tick double spec performs identically at any Attack level. Your close game is as strong as any G Maul build.
  • 03
    Fight in the lower bracket — that's the point. You're ~4 CB below a 60 Attack zerker. Opponents at your CB level are weaker. Use the bracket advantage.
  • 04
    Consider upgrading to 60 Attack for Dragon Scimitar. The 4 combat levels cost of going 50→60 Attack is worth it for the Dragon Scimitar's significantly better accuracy. The 50 Atk bracket is narrow.
