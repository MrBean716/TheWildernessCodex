Void Tribrid

All three Void helms — Melee, Ranger, and Mage — full tribrid on a Void framework

The Void Tribrid uses all three Void helmets — Melee, Ranger, and Mage — cycling between them mid-fight. The Void Mage Helm's +45% magic accuracy makes Ice Barrage freezes land reliably without heavy Magic investment, and the freeze converts into free melee or ranged hits while the opponent is locked in place.

The Void Tribrid is the most mechanically demanding Void build — but produces the most powerful output. Freezing an opponent, switching to Void Melee Helm for free Whip hits while they're frozen, then responding to their prayer update with Blowpipe or a re-freeze creates a damage pattern no single protection prayer can block.

The Void Mage Helm's +45% magic accuracy applies even through Protect from Magic — the spell deals 0 damage but the FREEZE still lands. This makes Void Tribrid freeze attempts far more reliable than other builds: even a protected opponent gets frozen.

Tips

  • 01
    Void Mage Helm freezes land through Protect from Magic. This is the defining advantage of Void Tribrid over non-Void tribrids. +45% magic accuracy means freezes are highly reliable even against protected opponents.
  • 02
    The freeze opener is your strongest sequence. Freeze → immediate Void Melee Helm swap → they prayed Magic so melee unblocked → 5 free Whip ticks. Practise the helm swap to be under 1 tick.
  • 03
    Full Void Pure guide covers hybrid fundamentals. See void.html before attempting tribrid. The two-style rotation must be automatic before adding the magic helm.
  • 04
    Vengeance + AGS is still the kill combo. Regardless of how the fight unfolds, it ends with Veng recoil + spec. Pre-cast during approach, re-cast after triggering.
  • 05
    Three helms means three inventory slots. You'll carry Void Ranger Helm, Void Melee Helm, and Void Mage Helm in your inventory alongside food and potions. Plan inventory carefully.
