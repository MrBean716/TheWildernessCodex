The Pure Philosophy

Why 1 Defence is the most mechanically demanding PvP build in the game

A 1-defence pure is an account that deliberately avoids training Defence, keeping it at level 1. The payoff is a dramatically low combat level for your offensive stats. A pure with 99 Strength, 75 Attack, and 99 Ranged sits at roughly combat level 75–82 — fighting opponents whose maximum HP is 60–75. Against those targets, your damage output is devastating.

The trade-off is total: you wear leather armour or black d'hide. A rune-armoured opponent hits you like you're wearing cloth, because you are. Every fight is a race. You must deal more damage faster than your opponent can eat through. If you lose that race, you die — there is no defensive fallback.

The Core Trade-off

Defence levels raise your combat level at a rate of 0.25 per level (via the defence contribution formula). By staying at 1 Defence, you keep your combat level as low as possible for your offensive investment. This is the foundational logic of the pure build:

  • Lower combat level = fighting opponents with lower HP and lower gear
  • Higher offensive stats = far more damage than those opponents can deal at their level
  • Result = asymmetric fights where you hit much harder than your opponent, but die much faster if they land hits
Defence levels cost exactly as much combat level as Strength levels — but Strength raises your max hit while Defence only reduces incoming damage. For a pure, Strength is always the better investment. Training Defence "wastes" combat levels on survivability when you could spend them on damage instead.

Why This Is Mechanically Hard

Playing a pure well is harder than playing a defence account, not easier. With 1 Defence you have no margin for error — a single bad tick where you fail to eat, miss a prayer, or mistimed a switch can end the fight immediately. Every mechanic that's important on a defence account is critical on a pure:

  • Combo eating is not optional — it's the entire survival system
  • Prayer timing must be precise — you have no armour to catch a missed prayer flick
  • Spec timing must be correct — specs at the wrong moment waste your only finishing tool
  • Matchup awareness is essential — a pure that misjudges who to fight dies in two hits
Do not start a pure as your first PvP experience. Learn mechanics on the beginner build first. A pure with bad fundamentals is just an expensive way to feed kills to better players.

Is a Pure Still Viable?

Yes — thoroughly. The pure meta evolves constantly but 1-defence builds remain among the most played PvP accounts in OSRS. The combination of low combat level and high burst potential makes them excellent in Bounty Hunter, low-level Wilderness, and the LMS (Last Man Standing) minigame. Pures also dominate in Edge PK situations where you hunt players weaker than your hidden offensive stats suggest.

Prayer Levels to Target

  • 13 Prayer — Burst of Strength. Tiny boost but no combat level cost until you hit higher breakpoints.
  • 31 Prayer — Protect from Melee / Missiles / Magic. The minimum for prayer blocking. Essential.
  • 44 Prayer — All three Protect prayers unlocked. Highly recommended.
  • 52 Prayer — Smite. Drains opponent's Prayer by 1/4 of damage dealt. Powerful in longer fights against prayer-reliant opponents.
  • 55 Prayer — Preserve. Slows stat drain from potions. Useful in extended trips.
Prayer adds 0.125 combat levels per level after 5. Getting to 44 Prayer adds approximately 5 combat levels — a meaningful but worthwhile trade for all three protection prayers. Most competitive pure builds stop here or at 52 for Smite.

The Granite Maul — Pure's Primary Finisher

The Granite Maul (g maul) is the most important weapon on a pure. Its special attack is unique: it fires as a zero-delay additional attack immediately following any other attack. There is no spec bar drain in the traditional sense — instead it uses a separate "instant attack" mechanic. Two g maul specs can be triggered in rapid succession by right-clicking and selecting "Use Special Attack" twice.

G Maul finish — from Dragon Scimitar
Scim auto-attacks
→
Equip Granite Maul (same tick as scim swing)
→
Right-click → Special Attack
→
Right-click → Special Attack again
→
Scim hit + 2× G Maul specs in 1–2 ticks

The power of this is the near-simultaneous nature. A 20 from the scimitar followed by 22 and 18 from g maul specs arrives so fast that even a skilled opponent cannot eat through it in time. From 60 HP, this three-hit sequence is lethal if the rolls are average or better.

The Granite Maul requires no Attack level to wield — it's a Strength-based crush weapon. Even a pure with 1 Attack can equip it. This is why it appears on virtually every pure build regardless of the melee stat focus.

Food & Potions

Eating on a pure is harder and more critical than on any other build

With 1 Defence, your HP is low (typically 70–80 at the pure's combat level), your armour provides almost no damage reduction, and your opponents hit you reliably. A single 30+ hit on a pure can take you from safe to critical in one tick. Food management is not a secondary skill — it is equal in importance to your offensive rotation.

Matchup Guide

How to approach every common opponent type as a pure

As a pure, recognising your opponent's build type in the first second of a fight is critical. Different builds require fundamentally different strategies. Getting this wrong — especially against Dharok's accounts or max mains — is usually fatal.

vs. Other Pures (Mirror Match)

The most skill-testing matchup. Both players have similar HP, similar offensive power, and identical vulnerabilities. These fights are decided entirely by mechanics — who combo eats better, who reads prayer switches faster, who specs at the right moment.

  • Prioritise prayer reading over auto-attacking. If they activate Protect Melee, immediately switch to MSB (i) spec.
  • Never spec at above 55 HP — they can easily eat through it and you've wasted your finisher.
  • Count their food if you can see their inventory (Bounty Hunter UI shows this). 3 food left = spec window.
  • Your 0-tick g maul spec is harder to eat through than a DDS. If both players have identical HP and food, the player who executes the g maul combination faster usually wins.

vs. Defence Accounts (60–99 Defence)

Favourable if you manage food correctly. Their higher defence means you'll hit less frequently, but at 99 Strength your hits still deal significant damage when they land. Their advantage is they can take 2–3 more hits before death. Your advantage is lower combat level = they likely have less HP than their gear suggests.

  • Use Protect from Melee throughout — their accuracy against 1 Defence is high, so protection prayer is essential.
  • Force them to eat by landing consistent damage. Their food is finite; your job is to exhaust it.
  • Activate Smite if you have 52 Prayer — defence accounts often rely heavily on prayer.
  • G maul finish works excellently when they're at 40–50 HP and food-poor.

vs. Dharok's Accounts

Do not engage. Run immediately. Dharok's set bonus at 1 HP can hit 60–70 on a single attack. With 1 Defence, you cannot survive this. There is no scenario where fighting a Dharok's user is correct unless you have overwhelming food advantage and can one-shot them first — which is nearly impossible on their 99 HP.

Dharok's accounts exist to hunt pures. A Dharok's hit at max effectiveness can exceed your total HP in one tick. The moment you see Dharok's armour, drink your stamina potion and sprint away. No fight. No exceptions.

vs. Ranged Pures (Toxic Blowpipe)

Medium difficulty. A ranging pure has fast DPS from a blowpipe (3-tick speed) and potentially high Ranged bonus. Your 1 Defence makes you especially vulnerable to fast-ticking ranged weapons.

  • Activate Protect from Missiles immediately when they open with ranged.
  • Close the gap quickly — blowpipe has no minimum range, but fighting in melee range means your scimitar can contest their blowpipe DPS.
  • If they switch to melee style, switch your prayer to Protect from Melee.
  • Their 1 Defence is equally vulnerable to your scimitar — your damage output is symmetrical.

vs. Mage Pures (Trident / Ice Barrage)

High threat if they freeze you. An Ice Barrage pure can freeze you in place for 5 ticks while dealing 28+ magic damage per cast. Frozen and immobile with 1 Defence, you're an easy target.

  • Activate Protect from Magic if you see Lunar or Ancient Magicks spellbook.
  • Keep moving constantly — moving every tick reduces the chance of a freeze landing (mage freeze has travel time and you can sidestep it with precise movement).
  • If you get frozen, combo eat immediately and pray appropriately.
  • Closing to melee range forces them to tank your scimitar — mage pures have 1 Defence too.

vs. Max Mains (126 CB, Full Gear)

Unwinnable. Escape only. A max main with Scythe of Vitur hits three times per attack at potentially 45+ per hit. Your 1 Defence takes full damage from each. Escape immediately upon recognition.

F-Keys & Interface Setup

Pure-specific hotkey layout and inventory organisation for fast switches

On a pure, speed matters more than on any other build. The g maul spec fires in a window measured in tenths of a second. Your ranged switch needs to complete before your opponent can update their overhead prayer. F-Key fluency is not optional — it is the mechanical foundation of competitive pure PvP.

Recommended F-Key Layout

F1
Attack tab / Special Attack bar. Used to activate g maul spec and MSB spec.
F2
Inventory. Where you click food, switch weapons (equip from inventory), and drink potions.
F3
Prayer tab. Toggle Protect from Melee, Protect from Missiles, Smite, Preserve.
F4
Spellbook. Used if you're running a magic spec weapon or Vengeance (advanced builds).
F5
Equipment tab. Useful for checking your current gear setup mid-fight.
Esc
Close current tab / back. Fast navigation aid.

G Maul Spec Execution in Detail

The g maul spec is a right-click action, which is slower than a left-click. The sequence must be drilled until the right-click menu opens and closes faster than conscious thought:

Scimitar swings
→
F2 — inventory
→
Left-click granite maul to equip
→
Right-click granite maul → "Use Special Attack"
→
Right-click granite maul → "Use Special Attack" again
→
Both specs fire

Alternatively, once equipped you can right-click the granite maul in the weapon slot rather than the inventory — some players find this faster. Practice both and use whichever feels more reliable.

Inventory Organisation

Pure inventory layout matters more than on a defence account because you're switching items more frequently and faster. Optimal layout:

  • Top-left slot — Sharks. First food you grab in an emergency.
  • Slot next to sharks — Karambwans. Right next to sharks for immediate combo clicking.
  • Fixed slot, always same position — Granite Maul. Muscle memory — your hand should know exactly where it is without looking.
  • Fixed slot — Magic Shortbow (i) and arrows (if using ranging switch). Two slots: bow and arrows in adjacent fixed positions.
  • Potions — Top row for Super Strength and Attack. You drink these once per fight, so exact position matters less.
In Clan Wars, practice equipping and speccing the granite maul 100 times without looking at your keyboard. If you have to look down to find the right-click, you're too slow. The spec needs to fire within 1 tick of the scimitar swing to maximise the burst window.

Right-Click vs. Shift-Click

Some players use shift-click to equip weapons faster (no right-click menu). However, the g maul spec requires a right-click to access the special attack option — shift-clicking equips it but does not fire the spec. Your workflow must include the right-click for the spec itself. Equip via left-click or shift-click, then right-click for spec.

Tips & Common Mistakes

What separates a dangerous pure from a free kill

The Pure's Ten Commandments

  • 01
    Never train Defence. Ever. Even one level ruins the account's combat level efficiency permanently. Check every XP reward before accepting. Some quests award unwanted Defence XP — always take the non-combat reward option.
  • 02
    Overheal with Anglerfish before every fight. With 1 Defence, starting at 75 HP instead of 92 HP can mean the difference between surviving a 40-hit and dying to it. Anglerfish are cheap — always have them.
  • 03
    G maul spec timing is everything. Speccing at 65 HP opponent with full food wastes your finisher. Spec when they're under 50 HP and food-poor. The window is narrow — be patient for it.
  • 04
    Combo eat at 40 HP, not 20 HP. On a pure, 20 HP is already potentially dead from a single hit. Your combo eat threshold is higher than on a defence account — start the process earlier.
  • 05
    Read prayer icons immediately. Seeing Protect from Melee above your opponent's head is your cue to switch to MSB (i) spec. Delay of even one tick can let them swap to Protect Missiles and negate your ranged attack too.
  • 06
    Stamina Potion every single trip. On a pure with no defence, running is your second best skill after killing. The stamina potion saves your gear set. It costs nothing. There is no excuse for not having one.
  • 07
    Know your matchups before engaging. See the armour, identify the build, decide in one second whether this fight is winnable. Dharok's, max mains, and max range tanks are not your fights. Leave.
  • 08
    Smite is a long-game weapon. If you have 52 Prayer, Smite turns winnable fights into definite wins against prayer-heavy opponents. The prayer drain compounds over time — use it on fights expected to go 60+ seconds.
  • 09
    The g maul is not a 100-spec bar weapon. It uses its own instant attack slot — there's no traditional spec bar to manage. But it still has a short recharge before the second spec is available. Right-clicking too slowly wastes the burst window.
  • 10
    Clan Wars before Wilderness, always. The g maul right-click combo, MSB prayer-reading switch, and combo eat all need to be drilled until they're instinctive. These three mechanics are the entire pure skill set. Master them in zero-risk environments first.

Fatal Mistakes

Training even one Defence XP. This is not recoverable on the same account. Always click "Dismiss" or "Decline" on any reward that includes Defence XP. Quests that award Defence XP that must be avoided include: Waterfall Quest (do combat option gives Defence XP — use a magic tablet instead), Monkey Madness (take non-combat rewards), Dragon Slayer II (unavoidable Defence XP — not for most pures).
Speccing at the wrong time. The g maul spec hits hard but does not end fights from full HP — your opponent will eat through it and your spec is gone. A pure without their spec finisher is just an account with bad gear and no defence.
Forgetting to pot. A pure without Super Strength does roughly 25% less max damage. At 1 Defence, that 25% is often the exact margin that separates a kill from a death. Pot first, every time, no exceptions.
Standing still against a ranging pure. A blowpipe fires every 3 ticks and hits reliably through your 1 Defence. Moving every tick doesn't block hits but it does maintain your melee-range engagement where your scimitar is competitive. Don't let them kite you.
Single-eating at critical HP. At 15 HP with one shark, eating brings you to 35 HP — which is still within one-hit range of most combatants at this level. If you have karambwans, always combo eat from critical HP. A single heal is almost never enough.

Where to Fight

Best locations for pure PvP at different stages of your account's development

Clan Wars — Essential Starting Point

Before any Wilderness PKing, spend significant time in Clan Wars drilling three specific mechanics unique to pures:

  1. The g maul right-click spec combo — equip and double-spec in one fluid motion
  2. MSB (i) prayer-reading switch — equip and spec before opponent updates their prayer
  3. Combo eating at the 40 HP threshold — not 30 HP, not 20 HP, but 40 HP for a pure

Don't leave Clan Wars until all three are automatic. The Wilderness will punish any hesitation.

Last Man Standing (LMS)

The LMS minigame is one of the best pure practice environments in the game. All players start with identical gear — no risk, real competitive PvP. Pure mechanics are heavily used in LMS. The practice-to-actual-Wilderness-fight correlation is excellent, and you can play dozens of games per hour.

LMS also awards Rogue's pieces and other cosmetics passively just from participation. Playing LMS while learning pure mechanics is productive even while you're losing constantly — you're earning rewards and building mechanical skill simultaneously.

Bounty Hunter

Bounty Hunter assigns you a target close to your combat level. For a pure at combat 75–80, you'll mostly fight other pures and lower-level defence accounts. This is the ideal live environment for practising matchup recognition and the g maul timing. You earn BH points — spend them on imbueing your Berserker Ring and MSB (i).

Edgeville Wilderness (Low Level)

Once comfortable in Bounty Hunter, the Edgeville Wilderness gives you more varied opponent types. At combat 75–80, you're fighting:

  • Other pures (60–80 combat) — your skill-test matchup
  • Low-level mains (70–90 combat) — usually gear-limited, favourable fight
  • Occasional higher-level mains who've wandered into your bracket

Stay in levels 1–10 Wilderness and keep the Edgeville bank within 20 seconds of your position. Restock quickly after fights — pure trips are shorter because your food supply runs out faster.

Revenant Caves

The Rev Caves (level 17–40 Wilderness) attract both pure PKers and defence accounts farming for Revenant loot. Fights here are more varied and often more lucrative. Only visit once you're confident — the deeper Wilderness means you face a wider combat level range and escapes are harder.

Progression Timeline

  1. While training the account — LMS for skill practice with zero gear risk
  2. Stats complete — Clan Wars to dial in mechanics
  3. Mechanics solid — Bounty Hunter with cheap gear (100k risk)
  4. BH comfortable — Edgeville low Wilderness with budget gear (300k risk)
  5. Edgeville experienced — Full gear Edgeville and Revenant Caves trips
Never take max gear on your first Wilderness trips. Pures with expensive gear (Amulet of Torture, Toxic Blowpipe, Fire Cape) make high-value targets. Gear up progressively as your mechanics improve and you understand your survival rate. Losing a 3M setup on a misread fight hurts far less than losing a 50M setup to the same mistake.

When You're Ready to Move On

The pure is a specialist build, not a stepping stone — most pure players stay in this bracket because it's genuinely fun and competitive. If you eventually want to expand into defence-account PvP, the skills you've built (combo eating, prayer reading, spec timing, matchup recognition) transfer directly to the Mid-Level guide and beyond. A pure who's mastered 1-Defence combat arrives at the mid-level build significantly ahead of where most beginners start.
