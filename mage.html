The Pure Mage

1 Defence, 94+ Magic — freeze, bind, and burst with Ancient Magicks

The Pure Mage is the most utility-focused pure build. Magic in PvP isn't just about damage — it's about control. Ice spells freeze opponents in place, preventing movement and escape. Bind and Entangle reduce mobility. At high Magic levels with Ancient Magicks, you can lock opponents in position while dealing 20–28 damage per cast and stacking burst spells before they can respond.

Pure Mage is unique in that it can prevent opponents from running — a capability no melee or ranged pure has. Against low-Defence accounts with limited prayer, a freeze + burst sequence can be lethal before the target reaches a bank tile.

Ice Barrage (94 Magic) freezes for 5 ticks (3 seconds) and deals up to 30 damage. Ice Rush (70 Magic) freezes for 5 ticks and deals up to 20 damage. The freeze duration is identical — Ice Barrage just deals more damage.

Spells & Staves

Ancient Magicks, the freeze toolkit, and weapon choices

Spell Progression

Ancient Magicks requires completion of Desert Treasure I. Once unlocked, you switch to the Ancient Magicks spellbook — your standard spellbook is inaccessible while on Ancient Magicks (you must switch at the Altar of Zaros or via items).

The Freeze Spells

The four ice spells (Rush, Burst, Blitz, Barrage) all freeze opponents for 5 ticks. After the freeze expires, opponents are immune to freeze for 5 more ticks. This freeze-immune window is important — if you freeze immediately on re-application, it will fail and the attempt is wasted.

After freezing an opponent, wait exactly 5 ticks after the freeze ends before attempting to re-freeze. Attempting during the immunity window wastes your cast and runes.

Rotations

The freeze-burst sequence and re-freeze timing

Phase 1

Approach & Freeze

Opening freeze
Close to cast range→Magic Potion active→Ice Barrage — freeze lands→Opponent frozen 5 ticks
Phase 2

Burst While Frozen

Burst sequence while frozen
Freeze active→Cast Blood/Shadow Barrage for max damage→Opponent can't move or escape→Deal 20–28 per cast
Phase 3

Re-Freeze Timing

Re-freeze after immunity
Freeze expires (5 ticks)→WAIT 5 ticks — immunity window→Attempt re-freeze→Success — freeze again→Burst continues
Counting ticks is essential for re-freezing. An early cast wastes runes and gives the opponent a free movement window. Count: freeze expires → 1-2-3-4-5 → cast.

Fight Examples

I
vs. Melee Pure — Classic Freeze+Burst
Victory
You (Pure Mage, 94 Magic, Ice Barrage) vs ScimPure (99 Str, 60 Atk, 1 Def).
ScimPure approaches. You Ice Barrage at close range — freeze lands: 24 damage.
Frozen. You cast Blood Barrage three times: 18, 22, 19 while they can't move.
Freeze expires. ScimPure starts running. Wait 5 ticks. Re-freeze: 26.
More bursts: 21, 20. They're at ~20 HP with 2 food.
Final Ice Barrage: 28. Kill without them ever landing a melee hit.
Lesson: The freeze prevented all melee contact. 5 casts during each freeze window dealt 84 damage before the target could respond. This is the ideal Pure Mage fight — frozen prey dealing 0 damage while you freely cast.
II
vs. Ranger — Close Range Required
Victory
Pure Ranger vs Pure Mage. Ranger has Protect Missiles. You close to melee range.
RangeKing activates Protect Missiles. All your spells: 0 damage.
You close to melee range. Cast Ice Barrage at point-blank — freeze lands.
Casts while frozen: 24, 20, 18.
RangeKing can't effectively kite while frozen. Re-freeze after immunity.
Final cast: 22. Kill.
Lesson: Protect Missiles was their answer — but only works at range. Closing to melee distance removes the prayer's effectiveness against your spell accuracy since you're directly adjacent. At melee range, their Blowpipe has no minimum range advantage.
III
vs. Melee Pure — Escaped via Good Prayer
Escaped
Opponent activates Protect Magic and you can't land damage.
PrayMelee activates Protect Magic before you cast. All spells deal 0.
You switch to a melee weapon if carrying one. If not — no damage options.
You Stamina Potion, sprint to bank. Combo eat while escaping.
Escaped. No gear lost.
Lesson: Protect Magic is a hard counter to Pure Mage with no answer other than a melee weapon switch. Always carry at minimum a cheap melee weapon as a backup. Against known prayer users, opening with a freeze before they activate prayer is the counter.
IV
vs. Prayer Tank — Pre-Prayer Freeze
Victory
Prayer user who activates Protect Magic — but you freeze before they react.
You close quickly and cast Ice Barrage before they activate any prayer.
Freeze lands: 27. They activate Protect Magic while frozen — too late for this freeze.
Burst while frozen — Protect Magic blocks damage. Freeze expires.
You switch to melee weapon. Scimitar hits through their Protect Magic.
Scimitar: 29, 24. They're low. Final Ice Barrage before prayer reactivates: 25. Kill.
Lesson: The pre-prayer freeze bought burst ticks before they activated. When Protect Magic blocked your spells, the melee weapon switch kept pressure on. The final Ice Barrage in their prayer reaction gap closed the fight.
V
Learning Death — Miscounted Ticks
Defeat
Re-freeze cast too early — immunity window wasted the attempt.
Opponent freeze expires. You immediately cast Ice Barrage — immunity window still active. 0 damage, no freeze.
They sprint away and combo eat during the window you wasted.
You chase but their run energy beats yours. Distance opens up.
Escaped. Your runes wasted.
Lesson: Early re-freeze attempts during the immunity window waste resources and give opponents free movement ticks. Count carefully: freeze expires → wait 5 ticks → then cast. Those 5 ticks feel like an eternity in a live fight, but casting early means 0 results every time.

Tips & Mistakes

  • 01
    Always count ticks before re-freezing. The 5-tick immunity window after freeze expires means any cast before the window closes deals 0 damage and no freeze. Count every time.
  • 02
    Freeze before opponents activate Protect Magic. The pre-prayer freeze buys you burst ticks at full damage. Prioritise closing quickly and casting before they react.
  • 03
    Always carry a cheap melee weapon. Protect Magic completely shuts down your offence. A Dragon Scimitar or even a Rune Scimitar in your inventory lets you continue fighting when magic is countered.
  • 04
    Occult Necklace is essential. The +10% magic damage boost applies to every cast. At 99 Magic with Blood Barrage, this is a significant DPS increase — never fight without it.
  • 05
    Rune management matters. Ice Barrage costs 1 Blood + 2 Death + 4 Water runes per cast. At 10 casts per fight, that's 40 Water runes, 20 Death runes, 10 Blood runes. Bring large stacks.
  • 06
    Keep moving to avoid melee contact. At 1 Defence, any melee hit is dangerous. Cast while moving — you don't have to stand still to cast.

Where to Fight

Bounty Hunter

The matched-level environment is excellent for Pure Mage. Your freeze gives you control over fights that other pures can't replicate.

Clan Wars

Practice re-freeze timing obsessively here. Counting 5 ticks between freeze attempts is the single most important mechanical skill of this build.

Edgeville Wilderness

Open terrain works well — you need space to maintain freeze distance and chase after freeze. Level 1–10 for teleport safety.

Desert Treasure I is required for Ancient Magicks (Ice Barrage). The quest awards Magic XP as a reward — assign to Magic only. Completing Desert Treasure I is the single most important milestone for the Pure Mage build.
