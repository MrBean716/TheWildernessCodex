The Void Pure Philosophy

Why Void Knight armour changes the rules of PvP at this combat level

A void pure is an account built around the Void Knight armour set — a unique set of armour obtained from the Pest Control minigame that applies a flat 10% accuracy and 10% damage multiplier to whatever combat style you're using when wearing all four pieces. Unlike every other armour set in the game, Void scales with your offensive stats rather than adding a fixed bonus — which means at 99 Ranged with 99 Ranged Strength gear, the 10% bonus applies to a very large base number.

The void pure is fundamentally a hybrid account. Its power comes not from dominating in a single style, but from being dangerous in multiple styles simultaneously — and switching between them faster than opponents can update their protection prayers.

What Makes Void Unique

Every other armour set provides static bonuses — Bandos gives +6 Strength, Berserker Helm gives +3. These add a fixed number to your damage rolls. Void works differently:

  • Void Melee Helm (full set): +10% melee accuracy and melee damage multiplier
  • Void Ranger Helm (full set): +10% ranged accuracy and +10% ranged damage
  • Void Mage Helm (full set): +45% magic accuracy (no damage bonus for standard spells, but large accuracy boost)
  • Elite Void Top/Bottom (upgraded): +12.5% ranged damage on top of the base set bonus, plus +2.5% magic damage

The key requirement: all four pieces must be worn simultaneously — Void Top, Void Robe Bottom, Void Gloves, and the appropriate Void Helm. Remove any one piece and the entire bonus drops to zero.

Elite Void armour is the upgraded version of regular Void, obtained by spending additional Pest Control points to upgrade the top and bottom pieces. Elite Void provides +12.5% ranged damage (vs +10% for regular Void) and is considered strongly superior for ranged-focused void pures. It does not affect melee damage — melee stays at +10% accuracy and damage with regular or Elite Void.

Is the Void Pure Hard to Play?

Yes — considerably harder than a zerker or 1-def pure. The void pure's power depends on style switching: changing between melee, ranged, and sometimes magic in a single fight while your opponent scrambles to update their protection prayer. Executing these switches within a single game tick (0.6 seconds) consistently is a high-skill demand. The reward is proportional — a well-played void pure is among the most threatening accounts in the 85–95 combat bracket.

Do not start a void pure as your first PvP account. The style-switching mechanics are complex and require strong foundational skills from simpler builds. Read the Beginner guide and ideally the 1-Def Pure guide first.

The Void Mechanic Explained

Exactly how the 10% bonus works, what it applies to, and when it's better or worse than alternatives

How the Bonus Is Calculated

In OSRS, damage is calculated by rolling a random number between 0 and your max hit. Accuracy determines how often rolls that would hit actually hit (versus being blocked by the opponent's defence). Void applies its 10% to both the accuracy roll and the max hit calculation.

For ranged specifically with Elite Void:

  • Ranged accuracy is multiplied by 1.10 (10% bonus)
  • Ranged max hit is multiplied by 1.125 (12.5% bonus for Elite)
  • This applies to the final rolled number, meaning all gear bonuses, potion boosts, and prayer contributions are already factored in before Void's multiplier applies
Because Void applies a percentage multiplier rather than a flat bonus, it scales up with the quality of everything else you wear. At 99 Ranged with Dragon Arrows and a Twisted Bow, the 10% boost applies to a much larger number than it would at 70 Ranged with a Magic Shortbow. This is why Void gets proportionally stronger as your stats improve — and why it's specifically paired with high Ranged levels on void pures.

Void Melee vs BIS Melee Gear

For melee, Void is generally inferior to Bandos + Torture + Fire Cape at high Strength levels. The 10% accuracy and damage from Void Melee is outperformed by the static +30–40 Strength bonus from BIS melee gear. This is why most void pures do not use the Void Melee Helm as their primary fighting setup — they use it as a switch for style-change situations or as a budget option.

However, at lower Strength bonus levels (budget gear, no Torture), Void Melee can be competitive or even preferable because the 10% scales with whatever bonuses you do have.

Void Ranged vs Black D'Hide

For ranged, the comparison is different. At 99 Ranged with Elite Void, the 12.5% ranged damage bonus from the full set significantly outperforms Black D'Hide's static defence bonuses. You're sacrificing ranged defence for ranged offence — which is exactly correct for a pure-style account whose goal is to kill before being killed.

The Four-Piece Requirement

This is the critical mechanical constraint of the void pure. The bonus only activates when all four Void pieces are worn simultaneously:

  • Void Knight Top (or Elite Void Top)
  • Void Knight Robe Bottom (or Elite Void Robe Bottom)
  • Void Knight Gloves
  • Void Knight Melee/Ranger/Mage Helm (whichever style you're using)

When switching combat styles mid-fight, you swap only the helmet. The body, bottom, and gloves stay on throughout the fight. This makes style switching significantly faster for a void pure than for other hybrid accounts — you change one item instead of four or five.

The single-helm switch is the biggest practical advantage of void in PvP. When a zerker switches from melee to ranged, they change helm, body, legs, amulet, and ring — five items. A void pure switches only the helm — one item. This speed advantage allows style switches inside a single game tick consistently.

Obtaining Void Knight Armour

How Pest Control works, point requirements, and the fastest strategy to get a full set

Void Knight armour is obtained exclusively through the Pest Control minigame on Void Knights' Outpost. There is no other source. You cannot buy it on the Grand Exchange. This is a significant time investment — obtaining a full Elite Void set takes hundreds of Pest Control games depending on your efficiency.

Pest Control Requirements

  • Combat level 40+ — minimum to participate in the novice boat
  • Combat level 70+ — to board the intermediate boat (more efficient)
  • Combat level 100+ — veteran boat (fastest points per hour, but often requires solid combat stats to contribute)
  • No quest requirements to play Pest Control

Recommended Purchase Order

  2. Void Melee Helm — Unlocks melee switching. (200 points)
  3. Elite Void Top upgrade — The biggest DPS increase per point. (400 points)
  4. Elite Void Bottom upgrade — Completes Elite set. (400 points)
  5. Void Mage Helm — Only if planning tribrid. (200 points)
Play on the highest boat your combat level allows — points per hour scales significantly between novice (1–2 points) and veteran (4–5 points per game). At combat 85+, veteran boat is accessible and dramatically reduces grinding time. Bring high DPS gear for Pest Control games — your contribution to killing portals determines whether the game is won.

Fastest Pest Control Strategies

  • Portal focus — Kill the portals, not just the monsters. Portals ending the game quickly maximises your points per hour.
  • Melee DPS on portals — High-strength melee with a fast weapon closes portals fastest. Use your Dragon Scimitar or Whip for PC games.
  • Veteran boat — If you meet the 100+ combat requirement, veteran boat gives the best points per game. If just under 100 CB, consider delaying PC until you reach it.
  • Stay near portals — Don't waste time running across the island. Pick the portal closest to the starting gate and commit to it each game.
Pest Control is a team minigame — you don't have to solo it. Joining a full boat where other players are also contributing means games complete in under 5 minutes reliably. Popular world hopping communities and FC chats maintain active Pest Control worlds.

The Blowpipe — Core Void Pure Weapon

The Toxic Blowpipe deserves special attention. At 3-tick speed (0.6 seconds faster per attack cycle than a whip), it fires reliably and consistently. With Elite Void's 12.5% ranged damage bonus, Dragon Darts, and Rigour prayer active, the blowpipe reaches max hits that routinely challenge well-armoured opponents despite their Defence levels. The poison effect also applies ongoing damage after the fight ends if the opponent escapes.

Blowpipe charges deplete at 1 scale per 2 darts fired. Bring at least 500 scales per trip to avoid running dry mid-fight.

Defeat
Setup: A fight that demonstrates the single most common mistake void pures make — getting locked into one style and forgetting to switch.

Instead of swapping, you keep firing Blowpipe through Protect Missiles. All hits land for 0. Six wasted ticks.
Opponent hits you freely for 28, 22, 31 while you deal no damage. You're at 21 HP.

The mistake: Continuing to fire through a protection prayer deals literally zero damage — it's not "reduced," it's zero. Every tick you spend firing into Protect Missiles while they fight back freely is a tick you're conceding. The void pure's power is entirely in style switching. A void pure that doesn't switch styles is just a player wearing weaker armour than their opponent and hitting for nothing. The moment you see a protection prayer — switch styles. Immediately. No delay.

Matchup Guide

How to approach every common opponent type as a void pure

vs. 1-Defence Pures

Favourable — your style versatility shuts down their strategy. A 1-def pure's plan is g maul spec burst while you're stuck dealing with their prayer. Your constant Blowpipe harassment forces them to eat and prayer-switch repeatedly, exhausting their food before their specs can land on a meaningful HP pool.

  • Open with Blowpipe — pure players usually activate Protect Melee first, leaving ranged unblocked
  • Your 42 Defence tanks their g maul spec better than 1 Defence would
  • Blowpipe poison applies after their escape — can finish off a fleeing pure
  • Never stay in one style long enough for them to prayer-lock you

vs. Zerkers

Even matchup — execution decides it. Zerkers have comparable offensive output and both builds can access Veng + spec. Your advantage is the Blowpipe harassment before they can settle into a prayer. Their advantage is marginally better armour and a stronger melee Strength setup in the Berserker Helm + Torso vs Void Melee.

  • Open Blowpipe — most zerkers activate Protect Melee, leaving ranged open
  • Once they switch to Protect Missiles, swap styles instantly — you can do it in one item vs their five
  • Save your spec for after they've burned theirs — same patience rule as all other guides
  • Anglerfish overheal is critical — their Dragon Claws spec can reach 70+ damage

vs. Med-Level (75/99/75)

Favourable with good play. Med-levels have Bandos and a higher base defence than you, but their combat style is more predictable (whip + DDS spec). The Blowpipe surprise opener consistently puts them on the back foot before they can establish their whip rhythm.

  • Their DDS spec is not prayer-bypassable — if they spec you in melee, you take full damage. Combo eat pre-emptively when they equip DDS
  • You fight 10–15 combat levels below them — they'll sometimes underestimate you. Capitalise on the surprise
  • Vengeance + AGS burst is your kill combo — their whip hits provide good Veng triggers

vs. Other Void Pures

Highest skill test — identical kits, pure execution. Both players will be switching styles continuously. The player who reads and reacts to prayer changes one tick faster wins every exchange. Pre-eat with Anglerfish and never skip the overheal — surviving their Veng + AGS by 2 HP because you started at 102 HP instead of 80 HP is the entire fight.

vs. Maxed Mains (126 CB)

Do not fight. Escape with Blowpipe run-and-gun. A Scythe with Torva hits three times per attack through your 42 Defence for 80–100+ combined damage per swing. Uniquely for a void pure, you can fire the Blowpipe while running — harass them slightly to discourage pursuit, but your goal is exclusively escape.

Tips & Common Mistakes

What separates effective void pures from players wearing expensive armour for nothing

The Void Pure Commandments

  • 01
    Switch styles the moment you see a protection prayer. Firing through Protect Missiles deals zero damage — not reduced, zero. Every tick you waste dealing zero damage is a tick they hit you for free. Style switch is instant on a void pure (one helm). There is no excuse for delay.
  • 02
    Never exceed 42 Defence. Dragon Slayer II gives large unavoidable Defence XP and is specifically incompatible with a void pure build. Many other quests also award Defence XP. Check every reward window before accepting. Verify before each quest completion.
  • 03
    Keep all four Void pieces on at all times. The set bonus drops to zero if you remove any single piece. Void Top, Void Bottom, Void Gloves, and a Void Helm must all be equipped simultaneously. Only the helm swaps between styles.
  • 04
    Anglerfish overheal every fight, no exceptions. The 22 HP above your max HP is the margin that decides whether Veng + AGS kills you or you survive to kill them. Against other void pures or zerkers, those 22 HP are frequently the entire fight.
  • 05
    Both Piety and Rigour must be active for the correct style. Blowpipe without Rigour is 18% weaker. Whip without Piety is 23% weaker. Switching styles means switching prayers simultaneously. F3 for prayer, F2 for inventory — practise doing both in the same tick.
  • 06
    Cast Vengeance before engaging, not at engagement. The 30-second cooldown starts at cast. Walking to your target counts as the wait. By the time you've exchanged opening blows, Veng should be ready to re-cast for a second trigger.
  • 07
    The Blowpipe surprise opener is your primary advantage. Most opponents activate Protect Melee at the start of a fight because most builds open in melee. Your ranged opener catches them unprepared. Don't telegraph it by equipping the Blowpipe too early while they can still see you approaching.
  • 08
    Don't spec until they're low and food-poor. Same rule as every other guide. An AGS spec at 90 HP opponent wastes your biggest finishing tool. Wait for the kill window: 60–70 HP, 2 or fewer food remaining.
  • 09
    Monitor Blowpipe scales constantly. Running out of scales mid-fight removes your primary weapon. Never leave for a trip with fewer than 500 scales loaded. Check the Blowpipe's charge before entering the Wilderness — not after.
  • 10
    Clan Wars before the Wilderness — and more Clan Wars than any other build. The void pure has the most complex combat rotation of any build in this codex. Three styles, one helm switch, Vengeance timing, prayer management, and combo eating all happening in parallel. Drill until every action is autonomous. This takes more time than simpler builds — respect the learning curve.
  • 11
    Tribrid freeze is a skill ceiling, not a starting point. Learn hybrid (two styles) to fluency before attempting tribrid. Adding a magic freeze switch while still fumbling the melee-ranged swap will kill you consistently. Tribrid is the reward for mastering hybrid, not a shortcut past it.
  • 12
    Know the difference between tanking and mistake avoidance. A void pure with 42 Defence can survive a few hits but cannot outlast a well-geared defence account in a prolonged exchange. Fights must be decisive. If you're trading hits without landing your style switches, you're playing a different build badly — not the void pure well.

Critical Mistakes

Firing through a protection prayer. Protection prayers completely negate the style they protect against. Zero damage, zero. This is the most common and most costly mistake on a void pure. Switch styles — immediately, every time, no exceptions.
Removing any Void piece mid-fight. Taking off your Void Top to equip a Bandos Chestplate, or swapping Void Gloves for Barrows Gloves, instantly drops the entire set bonus to zero. The set bonus is worth more than any individual piece substitution. Keep all four pieces on.
Opening with melee against melee-prayer opponents. If you close the gap and engage with a whip, you'll face Protect from Melee instantly. You've wasted the surprise advantage that makes the void pure effective. Open ranged — let them establish the wrong prayer, then switch.
Skipping the overheal. The void pure's most common death scenario is taking a Veng + spec burst that lands for 73–80 damage. At 80 HP (base), this is lethal. At 102 HP (Anglerfish), it leaves you at 22–29 HP — survivable with a combo eat. The Anglerfish costs almost nothing. Never skip it.
Running out of Blowpipe scales in the Wilderness. With no scales, the Blowpipe is unusable and you're left with melee only — a significantly weaker void pure. Check your scales before every trip. Every time.

Where to Fight

Optimal locations for void pure PvP and the correct progression from practice to high-stakes combat

Before Anything Else — Pest Control

You cannot play a void pure without the void armour. Before any PvP consideration, you need to complete your Pest Control grind. Prioritise in this order:

  1. Regular Void Ranger Helm + Void Top + Void Bottom + Void Gloves (the first 750 points)
  2. Void Melee Helm (200 points — unlocks melee switching)
  3. Elite Void Top and Bottom upgrades (800 points — critical DPS upgrade)
  4. Void Mage Helm (200 points — only if planning tribrid)

Until Elite Void is complete, your ranged damage is 2.5% lower than the build's potential. It's worth completing before serious PKing begins.

Clan Wars — Non-Negotiable Practice

The void pure has the most complex combat rotation of any account in this codex. Before entering the Wilderness, drill these four sequences until they require zero conscious thought:

  • Blowpipe → see Protect Missiles → Void Melee Helm + Whip switch in one tick
  • Melee → see Protect Melee → Void Ranger Helm + Blowpipe switch in one tick
  • Veng trigger → AGS spec on same/next tick
  • Combo eat at 35 HP while maintaining style awareness

Spend significantly more time in Clan Wars than with other builds. The void pure is unforgiving — every mistake is immediately punished by a player who understands that protection prayers deal zero damage to the appropriate style.

Last Man Standing (LMS)

Standardised gear removes the gear variable. LMS is excellent for drilling style switching in real combat without equipment risk. Players use void and hybrid builds extensively in LMS — the practice directly transfers to Wilderness void pure play.

Bounty Hunter

Matched targets, structured stakes, BH points for imbuing rings. Start here with budget gear (500k risk) once Clan Wars mechanics are solid. BH is excellent for practicing the Blowpipe surprise opener in a live environment where the stakes are low enough to iterate and learn from mistakes.

Edgeville Wilderness

At combat 88–92, you'll encounter other void pures, zerkers, and mid-level accounts regularly. The style switching advantage is most visible here — opponents aren't expecting three-style pressure from a sub-100 combat account. Stay in levels 1–10 initially. Edgeville bank is close for fast restocking.

Revenant Caves and Wilderness Bosses

The void pure's multi-style capability makes it excellent for hunting PvM players who are focused on boss mechanics rather than PvP. The Blowpipe's fast DPS closes fights quickly against unprepared opponents carrying boss loot. The Wilderness Bosses area (Chaos Elemental, Callisto/Artio, Venenatis/Spindel) is profitable hunting ground at higher Wilderness levels.
