The 75 Attack Pure

The highest-output 1-Defence pure — Abyssal Whip accuracy meets AGS or Dragon Claws spec power

The 75 Attack Pure pushes the 1-Defence build to its limits. 75 Attack unlocks the Abyssal Whip — with its +82 Slash attack bonus, the whip is dramatically more accurate than the Dragon Scimitar, meaning far more hits land during the grind phase. The spec weapon options also improve: the Armadyl Godsword (AGS) becomes accessible at 75 Attack, capable of single-hit specs exceeding 70 damage.

The trade-off is a higher combat level (~85–92 depending on Prayer and other stats). You're now fighting other high-Attack pures, void pures, and lower zerkers — a more competitive bracket where your greater accuracy and spec power compensate for being near the top of the 1-Defence tier.

The Abyssal Whip at 75 Attack with 99 Strength and Super Attack + Piety (70 Prayer) produces one of the highest DPS outputs available on a 1-Defence account — you'll hit more often and for more damage than the Dragon Scimitar, significantly shortening the grind phase.

Fight Examples

Five fights showing the 75 Attack Pure at full capability

I
vs. 60 Atk Pure — Whip Accuracy Decides It
Victory
You (75 Atk, Whip, AGS, combat 88) vs ScimFighter (60 Atk, Scimitar, Claws, combat 80).
You pot up, engage. Whip: 31, 28, 35. Three hits land consecutively — whip accuracy at work.
ScimFighter scimitar misses twice before landing 19. Their lower accuracy is showing.
They've eaten 4 sharks. ~45 HP with 2 food left.
You AGS spec: 61. Kill from 45 HP.
Lesson: The whip's superior accuracy landed three consecutive hits while their scimitar missed twice. By the time the AGS spec fired, the accuracy gap had already decided the food economy.
II
vs. Zerker — Veng+AGS Stack
Victory
You (75 Atk Pure, 94 Magic, Veng available, combat 90) vs ZerkMaster (45 Def, Dragon Claws, combat 88).
Veng cast during approach. Fight begins — whip trading with their scimitar.
ZerkMaster Dragon Claws spec: 27+13+6+6 = 52. Veng recoils 39 back at them. You combo eat.
ZerkMaster at ~40 HP from the recoil alone. No spec bar left.
You AGS spec: 53. Kill. Veng 39 + AGS 53 = 92 total burst.
Lesson: Vengeance converted their Dragon Claws spec into a death sentence. They dealt 52 damage to you (survived via combo eat) while taking 39 recoil. Your AGS finished what Veng started. This is the defining play pattern of the 75 Attack Pure with Veng.
III
vs. Void Pure — Whip Cuts Through
Victory
You (combat 90) vs VoidHybrid (combat 88, Elite Void, Blowpipe).
VoidHybrid opens Blowpipe. You activate Protect Missiles immediately.
You close gap. Whip: 34. They switch to Void Melee Helm.
VoidHybrid whips for 28. You combo eat.
Extended exchange — their style switching is fast but your Whip consistency is higher than their Blowpipe through Protect Missiles.
You Veng triggers on their hit. AGS: 59. Veng recoil: 21. 80 total. Kill from 62 HP.
Lesson: The Void Pure's single-helm switch is fast but your Whip's +82 accuracy means more hits land regardless of their prayer state. The Veng+AGS close was decisive — they couldn't eat through 80 combined burst damage from 62 HP.
IV
vs. Med-Level — Outpunching
Victory
You (1-Def, combat 90) vs BandosPKer (75/99/75, combat 104, Bandos).
MSB opener before they activate prayer: 19+23. Down from 99 HP to 57 HP before they react.
BandosPKer activates Protect Missiles. You switch to Whip — scimitar would be here too but Whip hits harder.
Extended whip exchange. Their higher defence reduces your damage but 99 Str + Whip still averages 20–25.
They DDS spec: 20+16. You survive, combo eat.
You Veng active. They hit 29 → 22 recoil. AGS: 55. 22+55=77 burst. Kill from 58 HP.
Lesson: The MSB opener dealt 42 unprotected damage before they could activate prayer — a fight-defining head start. The Veng+AGS close finished a fight that was already decided by food economy before the spec landed.
V
Learning Death — AGS Too Early
Defeat
AGS spec at 85 HP opponent — the most common 75 Atk Pure mistake.
You AGS spec at 85 HP with 9 food: 44. They combo eat — back to 80+ HP.
No spec bar. Trading whip hits. You have 6 food, they have 7.
Opponent Dragon Claws spec at 40 HP: 29+14+7+7 = 57. Dead.
Lesson: AGS at 85 HP with full food was wasted. They ate through 44 damage trivially. The window for AGS is 55–65 HP with food nearly gone. Same 44-damage spec from 60 HP with 1 food would have killed cleanly. Never spec above 65 HP on a stocked opponent.

Tips & Mistakes

  • 01
    Piety (70 Prayer) is essential. At 75 Attack, Piety's +20% Attack and +23% Strength stack multiplicatively with the Whip's high base accuracy to produce noticeably higher DPS than any lower-Attack pure.
  • 02
    Vengeance + AGS is the signature kill combo. If you have 94 Magic, cast Veng before every fight. The recoil + spec in one burst produces damage no opponent can realistically eat through.
  • 03
    AGS spec window: 55–65 HP, last food gone. The AGS is a boom-bust spec — a lucky 70+ hit one-shots; a bad 30 hit wastes your finisher. Time it for when they're vulnerable.
  • 04
    Never spec above 65 HP opponent with food. Even a 60-damage AGS gets eaten through from 85 HP. Wait for the kill window.
  • 05
    MSB opener is your most powerful pre-fight move. Most opponents activate Protect Melee at fight start — an MSB spec before they react deals 35–45 unprotected damage before the fight has even started.
  • 06
    Whip accuracy pays off over long grinds. 75 Attack + Whip vs 60 Attack + Scimitar means more consecutive hits landing. In a 10-hit exchange, you might land 8 vs their 5. That's 3 extra hits worth of damage — significant.

Where to Fight

Bounty Hunter

Best competitive environment. Bracket at combat 85–90 includes zerkers, void pures, and strong 60 Attack pures. Rich matchups for practising the Veng+AGS combo.

Edgeville Wilderness

Occasionally fights mid-level accounts here too — great targets for the MSB opener strategy. Stay level 1–20 for teleport safety.

Revenant Caves

At combat 88–92, you're strong enough to contest the Rev Cave bracket. PvM farmers carrying valuable loot — MSB opener then Whip grind and AGS close.

At 75 Attack your combat level (85–92) means you occasionally see early endgame accounts. The Veng+AGS burst is your equaliser — don't be afraid of the bracket, but always assess gear before engaging.
