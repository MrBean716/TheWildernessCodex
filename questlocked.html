Quest-Locked PvP Builds

Controlling XP, unlocks, and combat level through intentional quest management

A quest-locked PvP build is any account that intentionally avoids, limits, or partially completes quests in order to control which skills receive XP, which gear becomes accessible, and which combat level bracket the account occupies.

This isn't a single build with specific stats — it's a design philosophy that applies to almost every optimised PvP account. Almost every build in this codex is, technically, quest-locked. Players just don't usually say "I'm quest-locked" — they say "this zerker can't do MM1" or "this pure is locked at 45 Defence." Same concept.

Quest-locking mistakes are permanent. Accidentally completing a quest that gives Defence XP on a 1-Def pure, or Prayer XP on a prayer-locked account, cannot be undone. Plan every quest on your account before doing it — or accept the XP as a deliberate choice.

Why Quest-Lock?

The three reasons every PvP builder manages quests carefully

1. Avoiding Unwanted Skill XP

Many quests give XP as rewards. Some of that XP goes to skills you're intentionally keeping low. Examples:

2. Unlocking Specific Gear Without Overshooting Stats

Quest-locking is often about what you want, not just what you want to avoid. Examples:

  • Complete Regicide (for Dragon Halberd) without the accompanying Attack XP from earlier quests in the chain
  • Complete Desert Treasure I for Ancient Magicks without allowing any of the combat XP rewards to push you over a stat cap
  • Stop Monkey Madness I partway to avoid the Defence XP while still accessing Ape Atoll for training

3. Reaching Exactly the Right Defence/Attack Level

Zerker builds need exactly 45 Defence — not 46, not 47. The only reliable way to hit 45 and stop is to use quests as one of the mechanisms for getting there, then permanently freeze combat stats.

Barrows Gloves Path

Recipe for Disaster — the most important quest for high-level pures

Barrows Gloves from Recipe for Disaster give +12 Strength bonus — the best gloves available in the game. Many PvP builds specifically quest-lock around completing RFD while managing the XP from its subquests.

Tips

  • 01
    Plan every quest on your account before starting it. The OSRS wiki lists every XP reward for every quest. Check it before accepting any quest reward.
  • 02
    Quests that give XP as a configurable reward are safer. Some quests let you choose which skill receives XP — always pick a skill you're comfortable training.
  • 03
    Some quests give XP automatically with no choice. Dragon Slayer I gives Defence XP unavoidably. Nature Spirit gives Defence XP unavoidably. These permanently end certain builds if completed.
  • 04
    Starting a quest is usually safe — finishing it triggers the XP. You can be partway through a quest indefinitely. Many pures start Monkey Madness I for training area access and never complete it.
  • 05
    Document your quest state. If you're running a stat-locked build, keep a record of which quests you've completed, which you've started but not finished, and which are permanently forbidden.
  • 06
    The OSRS wiki Quest XP table is your best friend. Every quest's XP rewards are documented. Cross-reference against your build's forbidden stats before completing anything.
  • 07
    There are no second chances. XP cannot be removed. If you accept Defence XP on a 1-Def pure, the build is permanently altered. The accounts that survive intact are the ones that planned carefully.
