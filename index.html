About These Guides

Why This Codex?

PvP guides online are full of fabricated mechanics, inflated damage numbers, and builds that require stats the account doesn't have. These guides are different: every mechanic is accurate, every damage range is realistic, and every fight example reflects what actually happens in the game.

The three guides form a complete progression — you can start at Beginner, follow through to Mid-Level, and eventually tackle Endgame when you're ready. Each builds on the last, so core skills like combo eating and prayer management learned early carry forward.

Whether you're dying to Edgeville pkers or planning high-value hunts in the deep Wilderness, these guides are written to make you genuinely better — not just to sound impressive.

⚔
Accuracy First
Every item requirement, stat threshold, and mechanic is verified against real OSRS game data. No AGS specs that "hit 72" on a beginner account.
☠
Honest Losses Included
Every guide includes a fight you lose — because learning from defeats is more valuable than pretending every fight is a clean kill.
🧭
Progressive Skill Building
Combo eating, prayer switching, and F-key discipline are introduced at Beginner and refined through each tier. Skills compound.
💰
Budget to BIS Coverage
Each guide covers multiple gear tiers so you can start fighting immediately and upgrade as you earn GP from kills.
· · ✦ · · All mechanics accurate to standard OSRS. Game tick = 0.6 seconds. Prices vary — always check the Grand Exchange before purchasing gear.
