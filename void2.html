Void Pure — Deep Dive

The full mechanics behind Void Knight armour in PvP

The Void Pure revisited: this guide covers the full depth of Void Knight armour in PvP — exactly how the percentage bonuses compound, how Elite Void differs from regular Void, and how the single-helm swap makes it the fastest style-switching account in the game.

Void Knight armour provides a flat percentage multiplier to both accuracy and damage for the matching combat style. All four pieces must be worn simultaneously. The only swap when changing styles is the helm — body, robe, and gloves stay on throughout.

VersionMelee BonusRange AccRange DmgMage AccMage Dmg
Regular Void+10%+10%+10%+45%None
Elite Void+10%+10%+12.5%+45%+2.5%
Because Void is a percentage multiplier, it scales with everything — your potion boosts, prayer bonuses, and gear Strength bonuses all get the multiplier applied on top. A 99 Ranged account with Rigour and Elite Void sees far more benefit than a 70 Ranged account.

How Void Works

The maths behind the 10% and why it's uniquely powerful at high levels

Most armour adds flat bonuses. Bandos Chestplate adds +6 Strength. Berserker Helm adds +3. These are additive. Void applies after all additive bonuses as a multiplier — meaning it compounds with everything else rather than competing with it.

The One-Helm Swap Advantage

When a zerker switches from melee to ranged they swap: helm, body, legs, amulet, ring — five items. When a void pure switches, they swap only the helm — one item. In a 0.6-second tick, you can complete the swap and fire before an opponent's prayer update resolves.

One-helm style switch
Wearing Void Ranger Helm→Opponent activates Protect Missiles→Swap ONLY to Void Melee Helm→Whip fires through Protect Missiles
The one-item swap is the entire reason the Void Pure is considered a top-tier hybrid build. Five-item swaps (like a zerker style switch) take 2–3 ticks to execute reliably. The Void helm swap is one item, one click, executed in the same tick as your next attack.

Fight Examples

I
vs. Zerker — One-Helm Advantage
Victory
You (Void Pure, 90 CB) vs ZerkerPro (85 CB, Dragon Claws). Identical specs but your style switch is 4 items faster.
Blowpipe opener: 25, 29, 22 before they activate any prayer.
ZerkerPro Protect Missiles. You swap Void Melee Helm + Whip in one tick. Whip: 33.
They switch Protect Melee. You swap back to Ranger Helm + Blowpipe instantly.
Dragon Claws spec after their bar is empty: 31+15+7+7 = 60. Kill.
Lesson: Every time ZerkerPro changed prayer you'd already switched styles. Their five-item swap took 2 ticks; your one-helm swap took one. You were always attacking with an unblocked style.
II
vs. Med-Level — Blowpipe Surprise
Victory
Med-Level (104 CB, Bandos) expects melee. You open ranged.
Med-Level activates Protect Melee. Blowpipe fires through it: 28, 31, 24.
They switch to Protect Missiles — you swap Void Melee Helm, Whip: 34.

Learning Death — Stopped Switching
Defeat
Fired Blowpipe through Protect Missiles for 5 ticks.
Opponent activates Protect Missiles. You keep firing Blowpipe: 0, 0, 0, 0, 0.
They hit freely for 27, 24, 31. Dead.
Lesson: Zero damage through a protection prayer. The entire Void Pure's value is the instant style switch. Any delay is free hits for them.

Tips

  • 01
    Never exceed 42 Defence. Dragon Slayer II, Monkey Madness reward choices, and other quests can give Defence XP. Check every reward before accepting.
  • 02
    Style switch is one helm swap — do it instantly. The moment a protection prayer appears, swap helm. No deliberation.
  • 03
    Keep all four Void pieces on at all times. Removing any single piece drops the bonus to zero.
  • 04
    Overheal with Anglerfish every fight. At 42 Defence, the overheal margin frequently determines survival against burst specs.
  • 05
    Cast Vengeance during approach, not at engagement. Let the 30-second cooldown run during your walk to the target.
  • 06
    Check Blowpipe scales before every trip. 500+ scales minimum. Running dry mid-fight removes your primary weapon.
