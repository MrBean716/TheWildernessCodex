The Initiate Pure

25 Defence — unlocks Initiate armour for prayer bonus while staying below Dragon armour threshold

The Initiate Pure is a niche build that caps Defence at exactly 25. This unlocks the Initiate armour set — a prayer-focused armour set that provides significant Prayer bonus at very low Defence. The appeal: prayer-intensive builds like Smite pures and prayer-flicking hybrids can dramatically extend their prayer uptime while keeping combat level low.

Initiate armour requires 20 Prayer and 25 Defence and provides the best prayer bonus available at sub-40 Defence. For accounts that rely on prayer — Protect from Melee, Piety, Smite — this extra prayer bonus translates to longer fight uptime before needing a Prayer Potion.

Core Strategy

Smite Drain

Activate Smite and attack. Every melee hit drains 1/4 of the damage from opponent's prayer points. At 99 Str landing 25+ hits, that's ~6 prayer drained per hit. Against opponents with 44 Prayer (44 points) running Protect from Melee, you can drain them in 7–8 hits.

Smite drain sequence
Activate Smite→Attack with Scimitar→Each hit drains ~6 prayer→Opponent's Protect prayer drops→G Maul spec finish

Tips

  • 01
    Defence cap is 25 — never exceed. 26 Defence locks you out of Initiate but still below rune armour. 25 is the exact sweet spot.
  • 02
    Smite is the reason to run this build. If you're not using Smite, the Initiate armour's advantages over Fighter Torso are minimal. Build your strategy around prayer drain.
  • 03
    Prayer Potions stretch further with Initiate armour. The +12 prayer bonus means each Prayer Potion dose restores relatively more of your total prayer pool compared to low-prayer-bonus gear.
  • 04
    Carry MSB (i) for opponents who switch prayers. Smite only works on melee hits — against prayer tankers who switch styles, a ranged bypass is still needed.
