The Max Main

99 in every combat stat — full access to every weapon, armour, and spell in the game

The Max Main has 99 Attack, 99 Strength, 99 Defence, 99 Ranged, 99 Magic, and 99 Prayer. At combat level 126, this is the highest combat level achievable in OSRS. Every weapon is available, every armour piece wearable, every spell castable. The Max Main's limit is gear budget, not stat access.

Max Mains are the benchmark all other builds are measured against. When a pure "punches above their weight" and kills a main, it usually means they killed a max main at sub-optimal gear or mechanics. A fully geared, fully skilled Max Main is the hardest account to defeat in OSRS PvP.

Fight Examples

I
vs. Pure — Asymmetric Defence
Victory
Max Main (126 CB) vs 75 Atk Pure (1 Def, 90 CB). They have higher DPS potential but you tank everything.
Pure's AGS spec: 52. You're at 69 HP — barely registered at 121 start.
Scythe: 41+27+14 = 82. They're at ~10 HP. Eating desperately.
TBow finishes: 44. Kill.
Lesson: Their AGS spec dealt 52 damage — significant in any other matchup. Against 126 CB with 121 HP from Anglerfish overheal you shrugged it off. The Scythe's 3-hit swing dealt more damage in one tick than their entire spec.
II
vs. Another Max Main — Pure Mechanics
Victory
Both 126 CB, similar gear. Fight decided purely by execution.
Both Veng pre-cast. Fight begins with TBow openers.
Extended tribrid exchange — both style switching.
Opponent misses a prayer flick. Scythe lands: 45+30+15 = 90 unblocked.
Veng triggers. AGS: 61. Veng recoil: 22. 83 burst. Kill.
Lesson: Max Main mirrors are entirely decided by execution. The missed prayer flick was the fight. At this level there are no gear gaps — only skill gaps.

Tips

  • 01
    Tribrid switching is mandatory at this level. Every opponent you face understands prayer. A Max Main using only melee is a 126 CB zerker — dramatically weaker than full tribrid play.
  • 02
    Infernal Cape over Fire Cape when possible. +8 Str vs +4 Str is a meaningful difference at max stats where every bonus compounds with Piety and Super Combat.
  • 03
    Vengeance is pre-cast during approach — always. At this level missing a Veng pre-cast means potentially losing the fight-ending burst that would have closed it.
  • 04
    Manta Rays over Sharks for consistent trips. At 99 HP your heal-per-inventory-slot matters. Manta Rays vs Sharks is a cumulative advantage across a long trip.
  • 05
    Practice prayer flicking at this level. Max Main fights are primarily decided by prayer management. Correct prayer flicking means taking 0 damage. A missed flick means taking 40+ from a Scythe swing.
